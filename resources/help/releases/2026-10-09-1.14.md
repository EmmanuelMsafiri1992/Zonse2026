title: CRM, expenses, petty cash, assets, loans, insurance, hiring, leave and projects get real rules
---

- **CRM & sales pipeline**: each stage sets a default win probability (lead 10%, qualified 25%, proposal 50%, negotiation 75%). A probability you set yourself is kept when the deal moves stage. Won deals need a value and lost deals need a reason. The home screen shows the weighted forecast, what was won this month, the win rate and the deals due to close this week. Reports cover the pipeline by stage, win rate by source and why deals were lost.
- **Expenses**: a claim needs an amount before it's submitted. It's approved or rejected in one click. Only claims paid with personal money are reimbursed, and only after approval. The same supplier receipt can't be claimed twice. Reports show spending by category per month, how it was paid and what can be billed to clients.
- **Petty cash**: a payout can't be more than the cash in the box on that day, and voucher numbers can't repeat. "Count the cash" records any shortage or surplus and locks the reconciled entries. The cash book report runs from an opening balance.
- **Fixed assets**: asset tags are unique. Assets depreciate straight-line over their useful life from the month after purchase. Each asset shows its monthly charge, accumulated depreciation and book value. Warranties ending within 30 days are flagged. Reports give a depreciation schedule, a breakdown by category and who holds what.
- **Loans & microfinance**: loans use flat monthly interest repaid in equal monthly instalments. Each loan tracks what's paid, the balance and any arrears, and moves between disbursed, in arrears and repaid by itself; a nightly check catches loans that fall behind. A repayment can't be more than the balance. Every loan prints a statement. The home screen shows the outstanding book, portfolio at risk and the loans in arrears.
- **Insurance management**: policy numbers are unique, and an active policy needs inception and renewal dates. A claim must fall inside the cover period and can't be made on a quote. Approved and paid claims can't exceed the sum insured. Each policy shows its loss ratio. "Renew for a year" rolls the dates forward with the new premium. Unrenewed policies lapse after their renewal date.
- **Recruitment**: candidates can only apply to open vacancies, and the same email can't apply twice for one job. No more people are hired than there are positions. Once every position is hired, the vacancy is marked filled. Reports show the funnel per vacancy and time to hire.
- **Leave management**: a request can't ask for more days than its dates cover, and one person's leave can't overlap. Annual leave is capped at 21 days a year, and each request shows what's booked and left. Requests are approved or declined in one click. Approved leave becomes "taken" once it's over. The home screen shows who's away this week.
- **Projects**: milestone billing must stay within the project budget. Milestones can't be added to completed or cancelled projects, and a project can't be completed while milestones are open. Projects show their progress and billed amount, and go active when work on a milestone starts. Overdue milestones are listed on the home screen.
