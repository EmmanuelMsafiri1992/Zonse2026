title: Drawings, practice management, surveying, job cards and solar installs get real rules
---

- **Drawings & document control** keeps one revision per drawing number at each status, so issuing a new revision for construction supersedes the earlier one and superseded drawings are frozen. Transmittals list drawings by number and only send drawings that are current, and a construction issue carries only drawings that are for construction. Home shows the register and transmittals awaiting acknowledgement; reports show the register by discipline, revision history and transmittals by purpose.
- **Architecture / engineering practice management** never lets fees invoiced exceed the total fee, logs time only on appointed commissions and in sensible amounts, and locks billed time. Each commission shows its hours, fee remaining and how much is invoiced, moves through the work stages one click at a time, and cannot be completed with time still unbilled. Home shows the practice at a glance and fees to invoice; reports show fees by client and hours by stage.
- **Surveying & GIS jobs** book field work only with a surveyor who is not already in the field that day, check coordinates are a valid latitude and longitude pair, deliver a job only with its deliverables listed, and lodge only cadastral surveys, each under its own diagram number. Home shows jobs in the field and due; reports show jobs by type and surveyor workload.
- **Contractor job cards** send a technician out on one job at a time, keep labour hours sensible, need a compliance certificate on regulated trades before a job is complete, and have every completed job signed off by the customer. Invoiced jobs are locked and jobs are billed with one click. Home shows today's jobs and technicians; reports show jobs by trade and technicians.
- **Solar installer** recommends a system size from the customer's monthly usage, quotes a survey with a price, and wins it at most once, scheduling the installation. An installation comes from a won survey, inherits its customer and contract value, is commissioned only with its equipment and serial numbers recorded, and gets a five-year warranty date when handed over. Home shows the pipeline and upcoming installations; reports show the survey funnel, wins by roof type and installations.
