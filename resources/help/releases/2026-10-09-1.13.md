title: Input shops, out-growers, tractors, silos, extension, vets and land leases get real rules
---

- **Agro-dealer / input supply store**: a product marks itself in stock, low (10 or fewer) or out of stock from its count. An already-expired batch can't be added. Credit sales need a pay-by date and the farmer's contact or phone. The home screen shows money on credit, sales past their pay-by date, products to reorder and stock expiring within 30 days.
- **Contract farming / out-growers**: grower numbers must be unique. A delivery can't deduct more than the grower still owes on their input loan, or more than the delivery is worth. Each grower adds up kilograms delivered, gross value, loan recovered and loan balance. Every delivery prints a payment slip with the net payable.
- **Tractor & machinery hire**: a job is priced at hectares × the rate per hectare. A machine under repair can't be booked, and a machine can't take two open jobs on the same day. The machine shows booked, in the field or available from its jobs, and adds up hectares worked, earnings and fuel per hectare.
- **Grain storage & warehouse receipts**: a receipt is refused for grain above 13.5% moisture, the wrong commodity for the silo, more than the silo has room for, or a silo being fumigated. A pledged receipt must name the bank, and can't be withdrawn until the pledge is released. Silo stock comes from the open receipts, and each receipt prints as a warehouse receipt.
- **Extension services & farmer training**: a visit can't be marked done without the advice given, and its follow-up must come after it. Training days can't count more women than attendees. Each farmer shows their number of visits and when they were last seen. The home screen lists follow-ups due and farmers nobody has visited in 90 days.
- **Veterinary & animal-health visits**: a farm call can't be marked done without a diagnosis, and its withdrawal date can't come before the visit. Farms still under milk or meat withdrawal are listed on the home screen. Vaccination campaigns start running on their start date, and complete after their end date once a count is recorded.
- **Farm land leasing & land registry**: parcel numbers must be unique. A parcel takes one active lease at a time, and land you lease in can't be leased out. A parcel shows as leased out while it has an active lease and goes back to vacant when the lease ends. Leases expire on their end date, and leases ending within 60 days show on the home screen.
