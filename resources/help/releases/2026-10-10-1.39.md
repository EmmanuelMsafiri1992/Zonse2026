title: Ads, digital signage, memberships, podcasts, agency job bags and brand assets get real rules
---

- **Ads manager**:
  - A campaign needs a budget to go live, and it can't end before it starts.
  - Impressions, clicks, conversions and spend are running totals that only go up. Clicks can't exceed impressions, and conversions can't exceed clicks.
  - Updating results works out the click-through rate and the cost per click, per thousand impressions and per conversion.
  - Each morning campaigns past their end date end, and campaigns that have spent their budget pause. Reports show results by platform and by objective.
- **Digital signage**:
  - Playlist slides are image links or short lines of text, one per line, shown for 3–300 seconds each (8 by default), and the loop length is worked out.
  - A screen goes online only with an active playlist, and a playlist that online screens are showing can't go back to draft. Screen names are unique.
- **Membership site & paywall**:
  - Tier names are unique and every tier has a price. Members join active tiers, once per email address.
  - Renewal dates follow the tier's interval, and lifetime members never renew. Renewing extends from the due date, and members lapse the morning after an unpaid renewal date.
  - Content needs a link before it is published. Each item shows which tiers unlock it.
  - Monthly recurring revenue counts yearly tiers at a twelfth.
- **Podcast hosting**:
  - Episodes take the show's next number unless one is given, and no two episodes of a show share a number.
  - Episodes move forward only. Publishing needs the audio file, its length and an active show.
  - Edited episodes go out on their release date each morning, and download counts only go up. Reports show each show's downloads and the top episodes.
- **Advertising agency job bags**:
  - Jobs move forward, except that client changes send work back from review and count as a revision. Approval needs the job's value.
  - Media bookings need the media owner and the cost before they are confirmed, and a billed job takes no more media.
  - Billing waits until every booking is confirmed, then adds the media and a 15% commission. Reports show jobs by client and media by type.
- **Brand asset library**:
  - Names are unique within each type, and the file has to suit the type, so a logo can't be a video.
  - Colour palettes list their hex codes, which are picked out and shown on the asset.
  - Approving is recorded. Retiring an asset keeps the reason, shown in reports.
