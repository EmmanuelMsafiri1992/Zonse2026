title: Immunisation registers, medical supplies, the patient portal and medical billing get real rules
---

- **Immunisation registers and community health workers**:
  - Each child is checked against the routine infant schedule: BCG at birth; OPV, pentavalent and PCV at 6, 10 and 14 weeks; rotavirus at 6 and 10 weeks; measles-rubella at 9 and 15 months.
  - Doses must be on the schedule and carry a batch number. A dose can't be given before the child is old enough, or twice.
  - A child's next dose date and status follow from what is still outstanding: up to date, due, or a defaulter four weeks after a missed dose. The daily job keeps every status current.
  - "Give due doses" records every dose that is due in one step. The home page lists the children to trace.
  - A CHW visit that ends in a referral must say where the household was referred.
  - Reports show doses by vaccine, coverage by village and CHW visits by reason.
- **Medical supplies distribution**:
  - The delivery deadline follows from the urgency (two weeks routine, three days urgent, next day for emergencies) unless one is set.
  - Orders are approved, picked and delivered. Cold-chain orders can only be picked with the cool box between 2 and 8 °C, and every delivery needs a delivery note.
  - Delivered and cancelled orders are closed. A cancellation needs a reason.
  - The dispatch queue puts emergencies first and shows late orders. Reports show orders by facility, lead time by urgency and the on-time rate.
- **Patient portal**:
  - There is one portal account per email address, and only active accounts can send requests.
  - Each request type has a reply target: appointments within a day; prescriptions, results and questions within two days; record copies within thirty days.
  - Staff take a request and close it with a reply. The reply time is recorded, and overdue requests show on the home page.
  - Reports show requests by type, replies by staff and requests by month, with average reply times and on-time rates.
- **Medical billing and coding**:
  - Claims need valid ICD-10 codes and must be submitted within ninety days of the service, unless another deadline is set. Late submissions are refused.
  - Remittances mark a claim paid or part paid and track the shortfall. Payments can't exceed the claim.
  - A rejection needs a reason. A rejected claim can be re-coded and resubmitted.
  - The home page shows drafts nearing their deadline and the amount outstanding. Reports show claims by insurer (paid, rejection rate, days to pay), a rejections log and unpaid claims by age.
