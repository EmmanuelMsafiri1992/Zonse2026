title: IT services, hosting billing, translation, recruitment, music studio and tattoo apps get real rules
---

- **IT services & MSP**: a managed client needs the number of users covered. Devices and credential notes can't be added for clients who have ended, and each serial number belongs to one device. Rotating a credential keeps the old note as rotated and opens a new current one. Each client shows its devices and the warranties ending in the next 60 days. The home page shows contract renewals and warranties due in the next 30 days.
- **Hosting & domain billing**: domain names are stored in lower case without "http://" or "www.", and each domain is listed only once. A domain expires a year after registration, and a hosting account is due a month after it is created. Overnight, expired domains renew for a year if auto-renew is on, or are marked expired if it is off. Hosting accounts more than 7 days overdue are suspended. Recording a payment moves the due date on and reactivates a suspended account.
- **Translation & interpreting**: the source and target languages must be different. Written work is priced as the word count times the rate. Every job after the quote needs a translator. Sworn translations must be proofread before they are delivered. The reports show the words translated by each translator and the work done for each language pair.
- **Recruitment agency**: candidates can only be put forward for open vacancies, and a placement needs the agreed salary. The placement fee is the vacancy's fee percentage of that salary. Placing a candidate fills the vacancy and rejects the other candidates still being considered. Each vacancy shows its candidates.
- **Music & recording studio**: a session needs a room, a start time and a length. Sessions in the same room can't overlap, and an engineer can't run two sessions at once. When a session runs over or finishes early, the charge changes to match the hours used. Projects move from pre-production through to release.
- **Tattoo & piercing studio**: a tattoo needs a deposit to be booked, and the deposit can't be more than the price. Work can't start until the consent form is signed and the client's age is verified. Finishing a piece shows what is left to pay after the deposit, and a free touch-up can be booked for six weeks later.
