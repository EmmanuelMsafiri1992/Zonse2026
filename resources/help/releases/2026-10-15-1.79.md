title: Zonseob AI, Claude paid for in your own currency
---

A new **Zonseob AI** page in the main menu gives everyone Claude, the AI model from Anthropic, for writing, planning, explaining, translating and everyday questions. It runs on Zonseob's own Anthropic account, so people in countries where a foreign-currency card is hard to come by do not need an account with Anthropic at all.

It is paid for with tokens. Every account starts with free tokens to try it; after that, buy a pack from the same page with Paynow (EcoCash, OneMoney, local cards), an international card, or a bank transfer or mobile-money payment our team confirms by hand. Prices are in US dollars and each pack shows what it costs in your currency at today's rate. Tokens belong to you, not a workspace, never expire and keep working when a workspace's plan has lapsed.

Every answer shows what it cost, and nothing is charged when Claude cannot answer. Chats are private to the person who started them.

The home and pricing pages now describe Zonseob AI and its packs whenever the platform's Claude key is set. There is a new help article, **Zonseob AI, powered by Claude**, and two new settings for the platform owner: `ZONSEOB_AI_WELCOME_TOKENS` and `ZONSEOB_AI_PACKS`.
