title: A chat on the website that answers visitors' questions
---

People looking at Zonseob for the first time can now ask their questions right on the home and pricing pages. An **Ask a question** button sits in the bottom corner of every public page and opens a small chat.

It answers from what is actually true today: the plans and their prices, the apps in each suite, the ways to pay and the help centre articles. Ask what Zonseob costs, whether EcoCash works, which apps suit a clinic or a school, or how to get started, and it replies with the facts and a link to pricing or to creating a free account.

When Zonseob's own Claude key is set the answers are written by Claude, which can follow up on earlier questions in the same visit. Without a key, or whenever Claude cannot be reached, the chat answers from the matching help article instead, so visitors always get an answer. Either way it says plainly that it is not a person and points to the support email for anyone who would rather talk to one.

Nothing a visitor types is stored beyond their own browser session, and each visitor can ask up to twelve questions a minute.
