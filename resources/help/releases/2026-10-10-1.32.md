title: Hotels, restaurants, food delivery, tours, car rental, bus seats and bars get real rules
---

- **Hotel & lodge**:
  - A room can't be booked twice for the same night, can't take more guests than it sleeps and can't be sold while out of order.
  - Stays are priced from the room's nightly rate. The balance after the deposit is shown, and an early checkout charges only the nights stayed.
  - Checking in marks the room occupied. Checking out leaves it dirty and books a checkout clean; finishing the clean makes the room ready again. Maintenance tasks take a room out of order.
  - Confirmed guests who don't arrive become no-shows the next day.
  - The home page shows occupancy, arrivals, departures and rooms to clean. Reports show occupancy, average rate and RevPAR by month, bookings by source, and cancellations and no-shows.
- **Restaurant & kitchen**:
  - Order items are written one per line ("2 x Beef burger") and priced from the menu. Dishes that are sold out or not on the menu are refused.
  - Dine-in orders need a table. The table is occupied while the order is open and left to clear once it is paid.
  - Orders go to the kitchen, come out ready, are served and paid, with tips recorded. The kitchen queue shows how long each ticket has waited.
  - Reservations must fit the table and can't overlap another booking within two hours.
  - Reports show dishes sold, sales by order type and kitchen times.
- **Food ordering & delivery**:
  - Orders move forward from received to delivered, and the time of each step is kept.
  - An order can't go out without a rider, and a rider carries one order at a time.
  - Riders must collect exactly the order plus the delivery fee unless it was paid online. Cash still with riders shows until it is handed in.
  - Reports show orders by channel, rider performance and on-time delivery within 45 minutes.
- **Tours & travel**:
  - Departures never sell more seats than the tour's maximum group, and bookings are priced per person.
  - A guide can't be on two departures that overlap. Cancelling a departure cancels its bookings.
  - Confirmed departures start running on the day and complete once they return. Each departure shows its manifest.
  - Reports show departures, travellers, load and revenue by tour.
- **Car rental**:
  - A car can't be booked twice for overlapping days or rented while in maintenance, and its status follows its rentals.
  - Handover records the mileage and fuel. The return checks the mileage, charges extra days and a refuelling fee for each quarter tank short, and updates the car's mileage.
  - Overdue rentals are flagged daily. Reports show utilisation and revenue by car, plus late returns, refuelling and damage.
- **Bus & coach seats**:
  - Each seat on a trip is sold once, and seat numbers must exist on the bus. Tickets are priced at the trip's fare.
  - Passengers board once the trip is boarding and the ticket is paid. On departure, paid passengers who didn't board become no-shows and unpaid bookings are released.
  - Cancelling a trip cancels its tickets and shows the amount to refund. Reports show load factor, no-show rate and takings by route.
- **Bar**:
  - Tab lines are written as "2 x Castle @ 25", and the tab total is always their sum. Rounds can be added to an open tab.
  - Closing a tab needs a payment method. Walk-outs go on the unpaid list until settled.
  - A VIP section can't be booked twice on one night. Settling the night charges any shortfall against the minimum spend, less the deposit.
  - Reports show takings by payment method, bartenders, best sellers and VIP minimum spend.
