title: Wedding planning, associations, social work and funeral services get real rules
---

- **Wedding & event planning (vendors)** keeps each client event's budget, guests and planner fee, totals what its vendors cost and what they have been paid, and tracks its checklist. Vendors are booked only on live events, with a deposit no bigger than their cost. Checklist items fall due before the event, and an event is completed only once it has happened and its checklist is done. Reports show events by month and vendors by service.
- **Alumni / professional associations (CPD)** gives each member one membership number, an annual subscription and a renewal date, and a member lapses by themselves a month after that date passes. CPD is logged only against active members and counts towards the year's target once it is verified. Home shows who is behind on points and who is due to renew. Reports show CPD compliance, CPD by category and members by grade.
- **Social work case management** moves a case from intake through assessment to active, referred and closed, with a risk level and a review date. High-risk and urgent cases must have a review date. Visits are logged only on open cases and completed only once they have happened. A completed visit with a referral moves the case to referred, and a case cannot close while a visit is still scheduled. Reports show cases by category and by risk, and visits by month and by type.
- **Funeral services & funeral policies** gives each policy one number, a monthly premium, a cover amount and a date it is paid until. Each premium moves that date on a month, and a policy lapses by itself two months after it runs out. A funeral is reported with the date of death, needs its documents before it is arranged and cannot take place before the death. When the funeral is settled, the policy's cover is drawn against the cost and the policy is marked claimed. Reports show claims, funerals by month and policies by plan.
