title: Pay only for the apps you add, and starter bundles
---

- **Add-on apps**: when your plan doesn't cover an app, add it on its own at its listed price. The price shows on the card before you add it.
- **Plan allowance**: Settings → Apps shows how many of your plan's apps you're using.
- **Apps & add-ons** on the billing page lists each add-on, what it costs and your total, with a button to remove it.
- **No surprise charges**: if a cheaper plan doesn't cover an app you use, you'll be asked before it becomes a paid add-on.
- **Describe your business** during setup ("I run a hair salon") and we'll suggest the best starter bundle.
- **Best-value plan**: the last setup step picks the cheapest plan for the apps you chose, counting add-ons.
- **Starter bundles in Settings → Apps**: switch on the recommended apps for any type of business in one go.
