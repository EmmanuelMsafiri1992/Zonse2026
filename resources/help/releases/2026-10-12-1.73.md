title: Readable app files in the full data export
---

The full data export (Settings → Audit log → Export all data) now has an `apps` folder. It holds one easy-to-read file for each app list that has records, such as `apps/payables-bills.csv`.

- Each file has the same columns as the list's own Export button: number, title, status, the contact, the amount and dates, then a column for each of the app's fields and any extra fields you added.
- Values are written the way you see them in the app. Choices show their labels, linked records show their titles and people show their names.
- Deleted records are left out of these files. `records.csv` is still included with every app record in raw form, as before.
