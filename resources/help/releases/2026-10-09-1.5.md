title: One inbox for email, SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram
---

- **Inbox**: messages from your support email, SMS number, WhatsApp number, Facebook page and Instagram account all arrive in one list. Read and reply without switching apps.
- **Connect channels** under Settings → Inbox channels. Each channel has a webhook URL to paste into your provider.
- **Linked to contacts**: a message from a known email address or phone number is linked to that contact automatically. You can also save a new sender as a contact in one click.
- **Teamwork**: assign conversations, filter by Mine and Unassigned, and close them when they're done. A new message reopens a closed conversation.
- **Notifications**: you're told when a customer writes in on a conversation assigned to you. Unassigned messages go to the admins.
- **Test mode**: try the inbox before connecting anything. Replies are recorded but not sent, and you can send yourself pretend messages.
