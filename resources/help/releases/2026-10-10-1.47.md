title: More retail apps get real rules
---

- **Retail pharmacy**: stock status follows stock and recalled products stay recalled. The scheduled-medicine register only takes scheduled products that aren't recalled or expired, and never more than is in stock. From schedule 3 up it needs the patient's ID and a prescription number. Dispensing takes the quantity off stock and a return puts it back. The home page lists medicines that are expired or expire within 60 days.
- **Hardware & building supplies**: quotes are valid for 14 days unless a date is set, and quotes that need delivery must have a site address. Expired quotes can't be accepted. Accepting a quote that needs delivery books the delivery. Deliveries only go out on accepted quotes and need a signed delivery note. The report shows quotes won by month.
- **Liquor / bottle store**: stock status follows stock, and a case must cost more than a single unit. Empties returns need at least one crate or bottle, and the deposit is worked out from the crate and bottle rates. The home page shows lines to reorder and this month's empties.
- **Mobile phones & electronics**: each IMEI or serial is stocked once, and 15-digit IMEIs must pass the check digit. Sold devices show when their warranty ends. Repairs on devices you sold that are still under warranty are free; other repairs need a price before they are ready. Repairs are promised three days after booking in, and the home page lists repairs past that date.
- **Vehicle dealership**: years and VINs are checked, and each vehicle shows its margin. Deals default to the vehicle's price and show the balance after deposit and trade-in. A deal at the offer stage reserves the vehicle so no other deal can take it. Selling marks the vehicle sold, and a lost deal puts it back in stock. Finance deals need the bank. The report shows sales and gross profit by month.
- **Bookshop & stationery**: ISBN-10 and ISBN-13 numbers must pass their check digit, and each is used once. Titles on order stay on order until stock arrives. Special orders are expected after two weeks, and the deposit can't exceed the total. When an order arrives or is collected, you see the balance due. Cancelling reminds you to refund the deposit.
