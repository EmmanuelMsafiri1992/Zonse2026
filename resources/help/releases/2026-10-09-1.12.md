title: Farms, herds, flocks, milk and ponds now keep their own books
---

- **Farm & crops**: a planting can't take more of a field than is still free, and fallow or leased-out fields take no new crops. Each planting adds up its activity costs and harvests, and shows tonnes, yield per hectare against the expected yield, and its margin. It marks itself harvested when the first harvest comes in. The home screen lists this week's field work and harvests due in the next two weeks.
- **Livestock**: tag numbers must be unique, and a mother tag must belong to a female already on the register. A treatment with a withdrawal period stops the animal being sold or slaughtered until the period ends. Weighings work out daily weight gain since the previous weighing. Dead or sold animals can't be treated or weighed.
- **Poultry**: a flock keeps one daily record per day, and deaths can't exceed the birds still alive. The flock tracks birds alive, mortality, eggs and feed. Layers show a laying rate. A day that loses more than 1% of the flock raises an alert on the home screen.
- **Dairy & milk collection**: a farmer can deliver once per session a day, and butterfat must be a realistic percentage. A delivery without a value is priced at the farmer's last price per litre. Rejected milk is worth nothing. Each farmer gets a monthly payout statement.
- **Cooperative & members**: new members get the next member number, and numbers can't be repeated. Received contributions add up on each member as share capital, subscriptions, levies and savings. Members who have left can't contribute.
- **Produce sales & grading**: a sale is priced at quantity × price per kg. You can't sell more of a lot than is left, or anything from a spoiled or reject-grade lot. A lot marks itself sold when it runs out, and goes back in store if a sale is reduced. Reports compare prices by market, crop and grade.
- **Fish farming**: each pond or cage works out fish alive, survival, standing biomass and feed conversion from the logs since stocking. Low oxygen, or pH or water temperature out of range, raises an alert. Only stocked ponds take logs and harvests, and the first harvest moves a pond to harvesting.
- **Greenhouse & irrigation**: runs on the same block can't overlap, and fallow blocks can't be scheduled. Fertigation above EC 3.5 is flagged. Runs left scheduled after their day are marked skipped overnight. Each block shows its water use over the last week, per m².
