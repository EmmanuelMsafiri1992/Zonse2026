date: 2026-10-11
title: Form errors stay on screen
---

- **All apps**: when a form can't be saved, the reason and everything you typed now stay on screen. Before, some browser add-ons (spell checkers, dark mode, password managers) made the page reload and lose both.
- **All apps**: "Move to" follows each app's rules, the same as editing the record. A haulage load can no longer move to Loading without a truck and driver, a legal matter can't be closed with unbilled time, and a ward can't be closed with patients in it.
- **All apps**: money details on a record show the currency ($100.00), and years show as 2018 instead of 2,018.
