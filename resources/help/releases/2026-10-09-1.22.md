title: NGOs, volunteers, memberships, ticketing, event registration and venue booking get real rules
---

- **NGO, donors & grants** moves a grant from pipeline to applied, awarded, reporting and closed. An awarded grant needs its value and dates, and it works out when the next funder report is due from how often the funder wants one. Beneficiaries are funded only by awarded grants and take the grant's programme. A grant cannot close while it still funds active beneficiaries. Reports show grants by funder and beneficiaries by programme and by location.
- **Volunteers** gives shifts only to active volunteers, on days they said they are free, with sensible hours. A shift is marked completed or missed only once its day has come. Each volunteer shows their hours, missed shifts and reliability, and nobody can be deactivated while they still have a shift scheduled. Reports show hours by month and shifts by location.
- **Memberships & clubs** gives every member a unique membership number. An active member always has a renewal date, a year after joining unless you set one. Members more than a month late to renew lapse on their own. Renewing moves the date on by a year and upgrading changes the tier. Home shows who is due to renew, and reports show members joined by month, renewals by month and members by tier.
- **Ticketing** sells tickets only for events that are on sale, never beyond capacity. Each ticket gets a unique code and complimentary tickets are free. An event counts its own sales, revenue and check-ins, and switches to sold out and back as tickets sell and are refunded. A ticket is checked in once, by its code at the door, and never for a cancelled event. Reports show sales by month and by tier.
- **Event registration** registers attendees for open events that still have places, once per email address. An attendee counts as paid only after paying the fee and can check in only once the event has begun. Registration closes on its own when the event starts. Completing an event marks everyone who never came as a no-show, and home lists the badges still to print. Reports show registrations by month and by organisation.
- **Venue & facility booking** books a facility by the hour, only while it is open and never twice for the same time. The charge comes from the hours booked times the facility's hourly rate unless you price it by hand, and a booking is completed only once it has happened. Each facility shows its bookings, hours and takings for the month and what is coming up. Reports show bookings by month and by type.
