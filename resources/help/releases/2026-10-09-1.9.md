title: Finance apps that do the sums
---

- **Accounting**: posted journal entries now build each account's balance, a trial balance, a profit and loss and a balance sheet. Account codes must be unique, and a posted entry can't be edited. Use **Reverse entry** to cancel it cleanly.
- **Banking**: each bank account keeps a running book balance. A reconciliation can only be completed when the statement balance agrees with the books. When it is, it ticks off every statement line it covers.
- **Payroll**: payslips work out gross and net pay from earnings and deductions, and refuse deductions larger than the pay. Each pay run adds up its payslips. Approving or paying a run carries its payslips along, and payslips print for employees.
- **Budgeting**: budget lines flag themselves over budget (spending) or under budget (income). Each budget shows how much of it has been used, and a report compares budget with actual line by line.
- **Tax & VAT**: a return's tax payable is its output tax less its input tax. Withholding certificates work out the tax from the gross amount and the rate. A return needs its submission reference before it can be marked filed, and returns turn overdue on their own after the deadline.
- **Purchasing**: turn an approved requisition into a draft purchase order in one click. The home screen lists requisitions awaiting approval and late deliveries.
- **Receivables**: debtor accounts read what the customer owes from their invoices, with an ageing breakdown. Accounts move between current, overdue and settled on their own, and print a statement of account.
- **Payables**: bills must be approved before they're paid. Payments can't exceed what is still owed, a cheque payment needs its cheque number, and the same supplier invoice can't be captured twice. **Pay in full** settles a bill in one step.
