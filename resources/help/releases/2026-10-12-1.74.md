title: Send app documents for signing
---

Any document template you print from a record can now be sent for e-signature in one step. This works for records in the apps, contacts and payments, for example a lease, a consent form or a bill sign-off.

- **From the record:** open the Print menu and pick the template under "Send for signing". The template is filled in from the record and attached as a PDF, and the record's contact is suggested as the first signer.
- **On the record:** a Signatures card lists every request made from it and where each one stands.
- **When everyone has signed,** an internal note is added to the record, and the signed copy and certificate stay under E-signatures as usual.
- Viewers can still print but can't send documents for signing.
