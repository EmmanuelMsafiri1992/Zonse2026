title: Plan limits for people and branches
---

Each plan now holds to the number of team members and branches it includes, the plan picker says which plan is selected, and the error pages carry the new brand.

## Plan limits are enforced

- The Team members and Branches pages show how many seats and branches your plan includes and how many you have used. Invitations still waiting to be accepted count toward seats.
- When a workspace is full, the Invite member and Add branch buttons become a link to Plan & billing, and any invite or branch sent anyway is refused with a plain message.
- Someone accepting an invitation to a workspace that has filled up since the invite was sent sees why and can try again once the owner upgrades.
- Workspaces without a plan, and plans marked unlimited, are never capped.

## Clearer plan selection

- On the onboarding and billing pages the plan you have chosen is now outlined and marked "Selected", so the current plan no longer looks the same as the "Most popular" plan.

## Error pages

- The 404 and other error pages now show the Zonseob mark instead of the old wordmark.
