title: Tutoring, parent portal, certificates, university, hostel and school transport get real rules
---

- **Tutoring & lessons** books lessons only for active students, keeps each lesson between a quarter of an hour and four hours, and never books a tutor into two lessons at once. A student cannot be paused or finished with lessons still booked, and each student totals their lessons, hours, fees and no-shows. Home shows upcoming lessons; reports show lessons by tutor and fees by month.
- **Parent & student portal** activates an account only with an email or phone to reach it, allows one account per email, and lets only active accounts send messages. A message is closed only after it has been replied to, and the portal tracks how long replies take. Home shows unanswered messages; reports show accounts by role, messages by type and reply time by month.
- **Certificates & verification** gives every certificate a unique verification code, made up when none is given, valid from its issue date until it expires or is revoked. A verification check looks the code up and records whether it was verified, not found, or a mismatch because the certificate is revoked or expired. Home shows recent checks; reports show certificates by programme and checks by month.
- **University & college** registers students on active programmes, one registration per student number and semester, bills fees at the programme's tuition by default, and graduates a student only with a GPA, which turns the registration into an alumnus. A programme cannot close while students are registered on it. Home shows pending registrations; reports show programmes by faculty, registrations by semester and alumni by year.
- **Hostel & boarding** counts a room's occupancy from its active allocations and marks it full when every bed is taken, so a student only gets a room with a free bed and a bed number nobody else holds. Exeats are approved with the person collecting the student, signed out and signed back in, and the hostel flags anyone not back on the day they promised. Home shows who is out on exeat; reports show occupancy by hostel and exeats by month and approver.
- **School transport** lets pupils ride only on active routes, logs a trip once per route and direction a day, never carries more pupils than ride the route, and totals each route's riders, fees and how often the bus ran on time. Home shows today's trips and morning trips not yet logged; reports show routes, riders by stop and trips by month.
