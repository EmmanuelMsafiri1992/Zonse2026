title: Your own fields and online forms
---

- **Extra fields on app records**: Settings → Custom fields now covers the records in your apps too, such as patients, pupils or vehicles. Add the details you need. They show on the form and the record page, can be required, are searchable and come out in CSV exports.
- **Forms**: build an online form from the new **Forms** menu. Pick what each answer creates (a contact, a helpdesk ticket or an app record), then add, reorder and require questions.
- **Share or embed**: every form has its own link, plus a snippet to paste into your website so the form appears inside your page.
- **Answers land in the right place**: a contact form updates someone you already know (matched by email) instead of adding them twice. Every answer is listed on the form's page with a link to the record it created.
- **Alerts**: admins get a notification when someone fills in a form. You can switch this off under notification settings ("Form answers").
- **Spam protection**: forms quietly block bots and very fast automated submissions. You can stop taking answers at any time.
