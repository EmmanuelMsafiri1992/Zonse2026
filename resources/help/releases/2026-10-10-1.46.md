title: Retail apps get real rules
---

- **Online store**: each SKU is used once, a compare-at price must be above the selling price, and products show their discount. A product with no stock shows as out of stock and comes back when restocked. Orders need a shipping address unless collected, and courier or post orders ship with a tracking number. Orders move paid → packed → shipped → delivered, and collected orders go straight to delivered. The home page shows orders to handle and low stock, and the report shows sales by month.
- **Multi-vendor marketplace**: commission is between 0 and 100%, and only approved vendors can have live listings. A listing with no stock is sold out. Suspending a vendor takes their listings back to review. Payouts work out the commission from the vendor's rate, happen once per vendor per period, and are paid only to vendors with bank details. The report shows sales and commission by vendor.
- **Product catalogue & price lists**: product codes are unique and every item needs a list price. A customer group has one active price list at a time, and lists expire after their end date. Each item shows its price on every active list. The home page lists price lists ending within 14 days.
- **Shipping & courier labels**: parcels need a waybill once they leave (unless sent in your own vehicle), and a waybill is used once per courier. Delivery is expected three days after shipping, late deliveries are flagged, and the report shows each courier's on-time rate.
- **Gift vouchers**: codes are kept in capitals and used once, and vouchers expire after three years unless a date is set. The balance falls as the voucher is used, and a voucher can't be overspent, used after expiry or used once void. Reversing a redemption puts the money back. The report shows vouchers issued, redeemed, expired and still owed.
- **Supermarket / grocery**: barcodes are unique, and an item's shelf status follows its stock and reorder level. Batches near expiry can be marked down below the shelf price, then sold or written off; both take them off stock and record the money lost. Batches past their expiry date are written off each night. The report shows expiry losses by department.
