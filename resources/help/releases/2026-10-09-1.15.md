title: Time tracking, documents, chat, meetings, calendars, wiki and approvals get real rules
---

- **Time tracking**: hours are worked out from the start and end times when both are given, and nobody can log more than 24 hours in a day. Each entry is valued at hours × rate, and non-billable work counts as zero. "Mark client's time billed" bills all of a client's open time at once, and billed hours and rates are then locked. The home screen shows this week's hours, the billable share and unbilled work by client. Reports break hours down by project and by person, and list unbilled work.
- **Documents & files**: a folder can't be placed inside itself or one of its subfolders, and archived folders take no new documents. An approved document needs a version, and approving a new version makes the older approved copy obsolete. Review dates can't come before the update date. The home screen lists reviews due in the next two weeks and documents waiting for approval.
- **Team chat**: channel names are unique, and a private channel must list its members. Archived channels take no new messages, and each channel can have at most ten pinned messages. Channels count their messages and show the busiest ones first. Reports show messages by channel per month and the most active people.
- **Video meetings**: meetings run between 5 and 600 minutes, and a host can't be booked into two overlapping meetings. Meetings start and end in one click, and the recording link is kept when one ends. A cancelled or ended meeting can't go live again. Meetings from past days that were left open are closed each night. Reports show meetings by platform and time spent in meetings per host.
- **Shared calendars & rooms**: an event must end after it starts. A room or resource that's out of service can't be booked. The same room can't be double-booked, and an all-day event takes the whole day. A room isn't booked for more people than it holds. Each room lists its upcoming bookings. The home screen shows today's events and which rooms are free right now. Reports show room use and events by month.
- **Knowledge base & wiki**: category names are unique, and article titles are unique within a category. A public article can't sit in a hidden category, and a category with public articles can't be hidden. A published article needs at least 20 words. Articles show their word count and reading time. Articles not updated in six months are flagged as stale, and reports list them.
- **Forms & approvals**: only active forms take submissions, and every question needs an answer. A submission with an approver goes straight to pending approval. Only the named approver, the workspace owner or an admin can approve or reject it, and a rejection needs a reason. Decided submissions are locked. Approvers see what's waiting for them on the home screen, and reports show average turnaround per form.
