title: Letters, certificates and receipts
---

- **Document templates**: Settings → Document templates lets you design your own letters, certificates and receipts. Start from a ready-made letter, certificate, payment receipt or blank page.
- **Fill in details automatically**: click a tag such as the customer's name, today's date or a patient's number to drop it into the text. When you print, each tag is replaced with the real detail, including your own custom fields.
- **See it as you type**: a live preview beside the editor updates as you change the wording, paper size, orientation, font, border, accent colour, logo and signature lines.
- **Print from where you work**: a Print button appears on contact pages, payments and app records such as patients or pupils. It opens a ready-to-save PDF. General notices print straight from the templates list.
- **Kept tidy**: switch a template off to hide its Print button. Each template counts how many times it has been printed, and every print is noted in the activity log.
