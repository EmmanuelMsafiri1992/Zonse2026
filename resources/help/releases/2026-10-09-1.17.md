title: Tenders, site diary, subcontractors, plant hire and snag lists get real rules
---

- **Construction projects & BOQ** prices each tender from its bill of quantities: every item is quantity × rate, the tender's cost adds them up and the tender price applies the markup on top. A tender is submitted only once it has items, closed tenders refuse new items, item numbers are unique per tender, and submitted tenders are marked won or lost from the record. Home shows what is being priced, what is out, this year's win rate and tenders closing within two weeks; reports show tenders by outcome and cost by tender.
- **Site diary** keeps one entry per site per day, never in the future, with the workers count and whether delays were reported. Entries go from draft to submitted to signed off, and a signed-off entry can no longer have its details changed. Home shows this week's entries, workers a day, rain days and entries waiting for sign-off; reports show labour by site, weather and delays & issues.
- **Subcontractors** track every subcontract's claimed, certified, paid, retained and remaining amounts. Claims can only be made against an awarded subcontract, are certified for no more than they claim and never beyond the subcontract value, need a certificate number once certified, hold retention at the subcontract's rate and are locked once paid. A subcontract on site with expired insurance is refused and its insurance is flagged thirty days ahead; a final account waits for open claims. Home shows the accounts and claims to pay; reports show subcontract accounts and claims by month.
- **Plant & equipment hire** keeps one hire at a time per machine, refuses hires on machines that are broken down or in service, and prices each hire from its rate basis: hours used, days, weeks or months. Returning a machine adds its hours to the hour meter once and sends it for service when the next service is due; meters never go backwards and fleet numbers are unique. Home shows the fleet, machines out and overdue returns; reports show utilisation by machine and revenue by customer.
- **Snag lists** raise snags only against inspections that have not been handed over, never due before the inspection, and verify a snag only after it is fixed. Each inspection counts its snags and shows how far through verification it is, and is handed over only once every snag is verified. Home shows open, overdue and awaiting-verification snags plus inspections ready to hand over; reports show snags by trade, inspections and time to fix.
