title: Catering, venues, travel agents, safaris, bakeries and butcheries get real rules
---

- **Catering & event food orders**:
  - Write the menu one dish per line, with an optional price per head ("Chicken stew @ 45"). The quote is the guests times the price per head until you change it by hand.
  - The kitchen sheet adds 5% spare portions. When staff is left blank, it is suggested from the style of service.
  - Jobs move forward from enquiry to invoiced. A quote needs a price, and confirming needs an event date that hasn't passed.
  - Quotes whose event date has passed are closed as lost each day.
  - Reports show jobs by month, the average per head by service, and quotes won and lost.
- **Venues & banquets**:
  - A space is never booked twice for overlapping hours; a booking with no times takes the whole day. Bookings can't exceed the space's capacity or use a space under maintenance.
  - Bookings are priced at the day rate, confirmed with a deposit, and show the balance to pay. A space with confirmed events can't be taken out for maintenance.
  - Past provisional holds are released and past confirmed events are completed each day.
  - Reports show bookings, days used, utilisation and revenue by space.
- **Travel agency**:
  - A flight can't be booked without its PNR, the ticketing deadline must fall before travel, and the commission can't exceed the selling price.
  - Bookings only move forward, and only cancelled bookings can be refunded. Bookings that miss their ticketing deadline are cancelled, and ticketed trips are marked travelled after the travel date.
  - Visas need a passport number to be submitted and are decided only once submitted. The home page warns about tickets due and visas still undecided close to travel.
  - Reports show sales and commission by type and supplier, visa outcomes by country, and cancellations and refunds.
- **Safari / camping / activity bookings**:
  - Activities never take more guests than their capacity per time slot, and campsites are counted for every night of a stay. Closed activities take no bookings.
  - Bookings are priced per person, and per night for campsites. Nobody is marked completed without a signed indemnity.
  - Each day, finished paid bookings are completed and past unpaid ones are cancelled. Today's guests and missing indemnities show on the home page.
- **Bakery & confectionery**:
  - A custom cake is confirmed only with a price, a collection date at least two days out and a deposit of at least half. The balance must be paid on collection.
  - Cakes move forward through baking, decorating and ready. Cakes left uncollected past their date are flagged.
  - Bake batches can't sell or waste more than was baked and go sold out when nothing is left. Reports show orders by month, flavours and waste by product.
- **Butchery with scale integration**:
  - Sales are always the weight times the price per kg. Scanning the scale's label barcode checks the check digit and that the weight matches the label.
  - A carcass's yield can't exceed its hanging weight, and the cost per kg is worked out on the yield.
  - Meat sold comes off the oldest carcass first, so each carcass shows what is left and goes sold out when used up; refunds put the meat back.
  - Reports show yield by animal, sales by cut and the gross margin.
