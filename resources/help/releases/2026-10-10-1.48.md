title: Consignment, classifieds, job board, handyman marketplace and chat-order apps get real rules
---

- **Second-hand / consignment / thrift**: each consignor's share must be between 0 and 100%, and only active consignors can bring in new items. Items go back after 60 days unless a return date is set. When an item sells, you see the consignor's payout and the shop's share; a sold item can't be returned or donated. You can pay out one item or all of a consignor's sold items at once. The home page shows what you owe consignors and items past their return date, and the report shows sales split by month.
- **Classifieds & directory listings**: a listing needs a description of at least 20 characters and a phone number or website. Each package sets the fee and how long the listing runs: free 14 days, standard and featured 30, premium 60. Approving a listing puts it live from today, and featured and premium listings show as featured. Renewing adds another run and its fee. Listings past their date expire each night. The home page shows listings waiting for moderation.
- **Job board / freelance marketplace**: postings close after 30 days unless a closing date is set, and close by themselves each night. Only open postings take applications, each email can apply once per posting, and bids are only for contract and freelance work. Applications move from shortlisted to interview to hired, and hiring someone fills the posting. Each posting lists its applications, and the report shows applications and posting fees.
- **Service marketplace (handymen)**: providers are vetted and then activated, and a request only goes to an active provider of the right trade. "Find a provider" picks the best-rated active provider with the fewest open jobs. A completed job needs its value, takes the provider's commission, and its 1–5 rating updates the provider's average. The report shows jobs, commission and ratings by trade.
- **Social commerce (WhatsApp catalogue)**: products with no stock show as sold out. Write order items as lines like "2 x Chitenge dress". Lines that match catalogue products price the order and come off stock once it is confirmed, and go back to stock if the order is cancelled. A paid order needs proof of payment, and dispatching needs a delivery address. The report shows orders by channel.
