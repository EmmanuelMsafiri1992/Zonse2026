title: Therapy practices, dietitians, rehab, home care and maternity get real rules
---

- **Mental health and counselling**:
  - A client can't start therapy or book sessions until consent is signed. A high-risk client must have a named therapist.
  - An attended session needs notes and can't be recorded before its date. Discharged clients can't be booked again.
  - Each client file counts attended and missed sessions. Three misses in a row flag the client as disengaged, and the home page lists them under "Clients to watch".
  - Reports show sessions by modality, caseload by therapist and attendance by month.
- **Nutrition and dietitian plans**:
  - BMI is worked out from height and weight. Every check-in updates the current weight, the change since the start and the BMI band.
  - A client has one active meal plan at a time: starting a new plan ends the old one. An active plan needs a review date, and the calorie target must be sensible.
  - The home page lists reviews that are due or overdue. Completing the programme ends any active plan.
  - Reports show each client's progress, the average change by goal and check-ins by month.
- **Rehabilitation and occupational therapy**:
  - A case needs goals before it becomes active or is discharged. Each attended session scores progress from 0 to 10.
  - The case keeps its first and latest score, the improvement and its attendance rate. Attendance under 70% is flagged.
  - The first attended session moves a case from assessment to active. Discharged cases take no new sessions.
  - Reports show sessions by funder and by therapist, and outcomes by condition.
- **Home care and elderly care**:
  - A carer can't be booked into two visits at the same time, and visits can't be scheduled in the past.
  - When a client goes into hospital or care is paused, their upcoming visits are called off and no new ones can be booked.
  - Carers start and complete visits on the day, recording tasks done and any concerns. The visit length is timed, and concerns show on the home page.
  - Visits nobody started are marked missed the next day. Reports show visits by carer and by client, plus a concerns log.
- **Maternity and antenatal care**:
  - The expected delivery date is worked out from the last menstrual period, and the gestation in weeks stays current.
  - Each antenatal visit records its gestation. A blood pressure of 140/90 or more marks the pregnancy high risk.
  - Recording a delivery closes the antenatal record. Births before 37 weeks are flagged as preterm, and births under 2.5 kg as low birth weight.
  - The home page shows who is due in the next two weeks and who is past their date. Reports show deliveries by mode (including the caesarean rate), birth outcomes and antenatal visits by month.
