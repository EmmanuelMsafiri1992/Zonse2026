title: Staffing agency, overtime, exits, org chart and health & safety get real rules
---

- **Staffing / outsourcing agency**:
  - A worker holds one active placement at a time, and the bill rate must be higher than the worker's pay rate. Placing a worker marks them placed, and ending the placement makes them available again.
  - Each weekly timesheet works out the client billing, the worker's payout and the margin, one per placement per week. Reports show the margin by placement.
- **Overtime, allowances and advances**:
  - Overtime is claimed after it is worked, once per person per day, and is approved before it is paid.
  - Each person has one live allowance of each type, and allowances end on their end date.
  - Advances are repaid by monthly deductions down to a zero balance. Nobody takes a new advance while they still owe on an earlier one, and the loan book report lists every balance.
- **Exit / offboarding & clearance**:
  - The last day can't come before notice was given.
  - An exit is cleared only once assets are back, IT access is removed and finance has signed off. Final pay is recorded after that.
- **Org chart & succession planning**:
  - A position can't end up reporting to itself through the chain, and one person holds one position. Positions show as filled or vacant from their holder.
  - The home page flags positions with nobody ready to step in, and the bench-strength report counts successors by readiness.
- **Health & safety**:
  - Injuries name the injured person, and incidents close only with a corrective action. The home page counts days since the last lost-time injury.
  - Replacing PPE closes the old issue and opens a new one with the same replacement interval. Toolbox talks record who attended.
