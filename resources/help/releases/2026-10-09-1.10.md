title: Savings groups, subscriptions and more finance apps at work
---

- **Savings groups & SACCOs**: members' contributions add up into savings, shares, social fund and fines. A member can borrow up to three times their savings and shares, less any loans they still owe. Loans add flat monthly interest, settle themselves once repaid and fall into arrears after the due date. Meetings total what was collected on the day, and members get a printable statement.
- **Subscription billing**: a subscription charges its plan's price times the quantity. On each next-bill date it raises an invoice and moves the date on by the plan's interval. An overdue invoice marks it past due, and paying up makes it active again. Trials turn active on their first bill, and retired plans can't take new subscribers.
- **Payment gateways**: each payment works out its fee from the gateway's rate, plus the net settled. A gateway reference can only be logged once. Disabled gateways take no new payments, and only a successful payment can be refunded. Test-mode gateways stay out of the totals.
- **Job costing**: jobs add up their costs against the budget and show the margin on revenue, or on the quoted price until there is revenue. Closed jobs take no more costs. **Invoice billable costs** bills the client at cost in one step.
- **Multi-entity consolidation**: inter-company transactions must run between two different companies. **Eliminate matched transactions** clears matched ones up to the period end, and a run can't be made final while anything in the period is unmatched. Reports show open inter-company balances and minority interests.
- **Inventory valuation**: landed costs add freight, duty and clearing to the supplier cost and show the uplift. **Calculate from stock** values every stocked product in Invoicing at its current cost price, and prints a stock sheet.
- **Treasury**: investments work out their expected return at simple interest to maturity, and turn matured on the day. FX positions carry their local equivalent at the booked rate. The home screen lists what matures in the next 30 days.
- **Hire purchase**: an agreement splits the balance after the deposit into equal instalments. It tracks paid to date and arrears, completes itself when fully paid, and refuses overpayments. It is marked defaulted once three instalments behind, and prints a statement.
