title: Gym, field service, legal, garage, laundry, security company and photography apps get real rules
---

- **Gym & fitness**: a membership renews one, three or twelve months after it starts, depending on the plan. Memberships past their renewal date expire overnight. Only members whose membership is active and current can check in or book personal training, and a trainer can't be booked twice at the same time. Freezing a membership adds the frozen days back when it is unfrozen. The home page shows today's check-ins and the renewals due this week.
- **Field service & job cards**: the priority sets the due date. Emergencies are due the same day, high priority the next day, normal in 3 days and low in 7. A scheduled job needs a visit date and a technician. A job can only be completed once the work done is described and the customer has signed off, and only completed jobs can be invoiced. The home page lists overdue jobs, and the report shows how many jobs were done on time.
- **Legal practice**: a time entry's value is its hours times the rate, and one entry can't be more than 24 hours. Time and court dates only go on open matters. A matter can't be closed while it has unbilled time: bill it or write it off first. Postponing a hearing books the new date. The home page shows court dates in the next two weeks, and the reports show time by matter and by lawyer.
- **Garage & workshop**: job cards only open on active vehicles, and the odometer reading can't go below the vehicle's last reading. Each job card updates the vehicle's odometer. A job card goes from booked to the workshop, can wait for parts, is marked ready with its labour and total, and is then collected. Each vehicle shows its service history.
- **Laundry & dry-cleaning**: orders are ready in 2 days, or 3 for dry cleaning and duvets. Express orders are ready the next day and cost half as much again. Every order needs at least one piece, and nothing is handed over until it is paid for. The home page lists late orders.
- **Security company**: an active site needs at least one guard, and occurrences can only be logged at active sites. Incidents and alarms are escalated as soon as they are logged. Closing an escalated entry needs a note of what was done. "Log patrol" records a patrol in one click, and each site shows its occurrence book and when it was last patrolled.
- **Photography studio**: a shoot needs a deposit to be booked, and the deposit can't be more than the package price. A photographer can't be booked for two shoots on the same day. Galleries are due 14 days after the shoot, or six weeks after a wedding. A shoot can only be delivered with a gallery link. Each shoot shows the balance still to pay.
