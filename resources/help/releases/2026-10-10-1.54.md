title: Taxi dispatch, boda-boda riders, coach operator, staff transport and haulage apps get real rules
---

- **Taxi & ride dispatch**: a driver with an expired licence can't take rides, and drivers whose licence has expired are suspended overnight. A ride can only go to an available driver, who stays on the trip until the ride is completed or cancelled. A completed ride records the company's share of the fare. The report shows the rides, fares and company share for each driver.
- **Motorbike / boda-boda riders**: an active rider must have been issued a helmet. Each rider remits once a day. A remittance that meets the daily target is marked paid; one that falls short is marked short and the shortfall is kept. Each night, a missed remittance is logged for every active rider who paid nothing the day before. Delivery jobs only go to active riders. Each rider shows what they remitted and still owe this month.
- **Bus & coach operator**: a charter can't carry more passengers than the coach has seats, and it must return on or after the day it departs. You can't book a coach that is in maintenance or whose roadworthy certificate runs out before the trip ends. A coach can't be on two charters on the same days. When a charter departs, its coach is marked as on a trip, and completing the charter makes the coach available again.
- **School bus & staff transport**: passengers can only be added to active routes. Each route has one morning and one afternoon trip log a day. The odometer can't go below the route's last reading, and a regular run can't carry more passengers than are on the route. The home page lists routes with no trip logged yet today.
- **Haulage & trucking**: the delivery date can't be before the loading date. A load needs a truck and a driver once loading starts, and a truck can't carry two loads at once. A load is only marked delivered with proof of delivery, and only delivered loads can be invoiced. Trip expenses are claimed, approved and paid. Each load shows its margin after expenses, and the report shows revenue, expenses and margin for each lane.
