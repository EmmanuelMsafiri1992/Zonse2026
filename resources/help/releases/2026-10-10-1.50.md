title: Call centre, van sales, customer portal, gift cards, warranty and service contract apps get real rules
---

- **Call centre**: an answered call needs its outcome, and a callback needs a time to call back. Missed calls and voicemails have no talk time, and you can log when you call them back. A campaign can't reach more numbers than are on its list. Planned campaigns start on their start date. Running campaigns finish when every number is reached or the end date passes. The home page shows today's calls and the callbacks due, and the report shows calls by agent.
- **Field sales / van sales**: an active route needs a rep, and only active routes take new visits and loads. A visit where an order was taken needs its value. Planned visits that aren't made are marked missed the next day. A route can have only one load out at a time. When you reconcile a load, the cash collected is checked against the orders taken on that route that day, and you see any shortfall. The report shows sales and cash by route.
- **Customer portal**: each email can have one portal account, and emails are stored in lower case. Disabled accounts can't send requests. Taking a request assigns it to you, and the report shows the average number of days to close each type of request. The home page lists requests that are still waiting.
- **Gift cards**: card codes are stored in capitals and each code can be used once. Gift cards and vouchers last a year by default, and store credit never expires. The balance goes down with each redemption, and you can't spend more than the balance. Reversing a redemption puts the money back on the card. Cards expire by themselves after their date. The home page shows the balance still owed to card holders.
- **Warranty & returns (RMA)**: each serial number has one warranty. A warranty runs 12 months unless you set another term, and it expires by itself when the term ends. A faulty or damaged item can only be returned while its warranty is in force. Change-of-mind returns are only taken within 14 days of the sale. Every outcome needs a note saying what was done, and a refund needs its amount.
- **Service contracts & AMC**: a contract runs for a year unless you give a renewal date. It shows as up for renewal 30 days before that date, and it expires once the date passes. Renewing extends it by a year. "Schedule the year's visits" spreads the visits evenly over the term. Completing a visit needs a report, and scheduled visits that don't happen are marked missed.
