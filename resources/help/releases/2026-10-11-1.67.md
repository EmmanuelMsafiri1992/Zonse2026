title: Approved and used amounts stay put
---

- **Gift cards**: a card's face value can't be lowered below what has already been taken from it.
- **Payables**: a bill's total can't be lowered below what has already been paid on it.
- **Leave**: a request asking for more days than its dates cover now says so, even when the person is also short of annual days.
- **Expenses**: a new claim can't be saved as approved or reimbursed; submit it, then approve it. Once approved, its amount is locked. To change it, move the claim back to submitted.
- **Payroll**: once a payslip or its pay run is paid, the payslip's pay is locked. A new pay run starts with no employees and zero totals, whatever was typed in. Its totals come from its payslips.
- **Budgeting, Investment clubs, Estate levy, Grain storage**: a new record starts its worked-out figures at zero, even if something was typed in. These are a budget's total, an investor's total, a venture's amount raised, an owner's balance and a silo's stock. They now come only from the records behind them. Before, a new venture with a typed "raised" figure could be marked funded straight away.
