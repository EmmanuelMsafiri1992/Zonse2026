title: Credit scoring, pawnshops, forex and more money businesses at work
---

- **Credit scoring**: an applicant can only be marked verified once their ID and proof of address are ticked. Each assessment puts the score into a risk band (excellent, good, fair or poor). Unless you enter a limit, it recommends one of three, two, one or no months of the applicant's income. Unverified applicants and AML matches can't be approved, and a possible match has to be referred.
- **Accounting practice**: a filing needs its submission reference to be marked filed, and turns overdue on its own after the deadline. **Set up next period** creates next month's VAT or PAYE filing, or next year's for everything else. Signed engagement letters expire on their renewal date. The home screen lists filings due in the next 14 days.
- **Audit working papers**: a paper needs a conclusion before it is reviewed or cleared, and its reference must be unique within the engagement. An engagement can't be reported while papers are uncleared or high-risk findings are still open. Archived engagements take no new papers.
- **Insurance premium financing**: the amount financed is the premium plus the finance charge, split into equal monthly instalments. Agreements go into arrears once a full instalment is behind, come back once paid up, and complete themselves when fully paid. Overpayments are refused.
- **Forex bureau & money transfer**: the sell rate can't be below the buy rate, and a new current rate retires the old one for the same pair. Deals work out the local amount and the margin against the board's mid-rate. Deals of 1,000 or more need the customer's ID. Transfers get a six-digit collection code, and a cancelled transfer can't be paid out.
- **Mobile-money agent float**: every movement updates the outlet's e-float and cash drawer. A movement can't spend more e-float or cash than is there, or push the float over its limit. It is only reconciled when the counted balances match. Outlets below 20% of their limit show as running low.
- **Pawnshop**: loans are capped at 70% of the item's valuation, and interest is charged for every started 30-day period. Payments go to interest first. A pledge is redeemed once nothing is owed, and can be extended for another 30 days when its interest is paid. It is forfeited 7 days after the redeem-by date, and only forfeited items can be sold. Each pledge prints a pawn ticket.
- **Investment clubs & crowdfunding**: investors' totals and ventures' amounts raised add up from contributions. A raising venture becomes funded at its target and takes nothing more. A paid distribution is shared among the venture's investors in proportion to what each put in.
- **Household budgeting**: a budget for a month and category adds up that month's spending in the category as transactions come in. The home screen shows the month's income, spending and anything over budget.
