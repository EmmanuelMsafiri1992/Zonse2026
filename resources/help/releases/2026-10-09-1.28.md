title: Patient queues, medical aid claims, laboratory, vets, appointments and medical records get real rules
---

- **Patient queue & tokens**:
  - Tokens are issued for today. Each is numbered with a letter for its service, such as C for consultation and P for pharmacy.
  - A token is called to a room or counter, served and marked done or as a no-show. Tokens left over at the end of the day become no-shows.
  - Home shows the queue today, who is being served now and who is next in line.
  - Reports show waiting times by service and tokens by day.
- **Medical aid & insurance claims**:
  - A claim needs a member number, at least one valid ICD-10 code and an amount.
  - A service more than four months old cannot be claimed, and a service date cannot be in the future.
  - A submitted claim gets a follow-up date. It is then paid, queried or rejected. A queried claim can be corrected and resubmitted.
  - A payment cannot be more than was claimed, and any shortfall is tracked to recover from the patient.
  - Reports show claims by medical aid and by month, and the reasons for queries and rejections.
- **Laboratory**:
  - A request moves from sample collected to processing to resulted. Results are required, and a resulted request cannot go back.
  - Results can be flagged as abnormal. Turnaround time is tracked, and late results are flagged.
  - Home shows the work queue and recent abnormal results.
  - Reports show requests by month, turnaround by urgency and requests by referrer.
- **Veterinary clinic**:
  - Each microchip or tag number belongs to one animal.
  - A consultation is completed only with findings or a treatment.
  - Giving a vaccination schedules the next dose, a year later unless another date is set. Doses not given by their due date become overdue.
  - An animal that has died can no longer be booked.
  - Reports show patients by species, vaccinations given and activity by month.
- **Patient appointments & reminders**:
  - Appointments are never booked in the past, and a practitioner cannot be double-booked at the same time.
  - An SMS reminder goes out the day before, so it needs the patient's phone.
  - Patients are marked arrived or seen only on the appointment day. Missed appointments become no-shows.
  - A free slot can be offered to someone on the waitlist, who can book it or decline it.
  - Reports show appointments and no-show rates by practitioner, by month and by reminder.
- **Medical records (EMR)**:
  - Each chart has one file number.
  - An encounter needs valid ICD-10 codes before it can be signed. A signed encounter is locked, and changes are made as addenda.
  - Allergies are highlighted on the chart.
  - Reports show the top diagnoses, encounters by clinician and encounters by month.
