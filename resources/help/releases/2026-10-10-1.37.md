title: Fuel stations, LPG cylinders, removals and towing get real rules
---

- **Fuel station**:
  - Tank stock is the last dip plus deliveries since. A delivery can't overfill a tank, and a dip can't read more than the tank holds.
  - Each dip records what the tank should have held and how much was drawn since the previous dip.
  - An attendant has one open shift at a time. Closing a shift takes the closing meter, price, cash and card or mobile takings, works out the litres sold and the amount expected, and marks the shift short if the takings come up short. Closed shifts are locked.
  - A delivery can be disputed with the litres actually found, and pumps can be taken out of service and back. Reports show takings by attendant and wet stock, including litres drawn from tanks but not sold.
- **LPG gas cylinders**:
  - Cylinder serials are unique, however they are typed. A cylinder past its test date can't be filled or go out to a customer, and condemned cylinders are locked.
  - A refill exchange or new-cylinder sale needs enough full cylinders of that size in stock. Saving the sale hands the cylinders to the customer and takes theirs back as empties.
  - Cancelling a sale needs a reason and puts the cylinders back. Cylinders can be filled, tested, condemned and collected. Reports show sales by size and cylinder stock.
- **Removals**:
  - A move only goes forward from enquiry to completed, and its from and to addresses must differ.
  - A survey records the volume and the crew needed for it. A quote adds goods-in-transit cover at 1.5% of the declared value.
  - Booking needs a date, a big enough crew and a truck that isn't on another move that day. Cancelling needs a reason. Reports show enquiries to moves and completed moves by truck.
- **Towing & roadside assistance**:
  - A call-out is dispatched to a driver who isn't on another job, and its response time is measured to arrival on scene.
  - Only tows and accidents are towed, and towing needs a destination.
  - Completing a job charges the call-out fee for its type plus a rate per kilometre towed, billed to the insurer if there is one. Cancelling needs a reason. Reports show call-outs by type, drivers and billing by insurer.
