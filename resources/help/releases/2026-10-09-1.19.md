title: Library, daycare, driving school, LMS, exams and timetabling get real rules
---

- **Library** lends a book only while it has a free copy, lets a borrower hold at most a few books at a time, sets loans due two weeks out unless told otherwise and marks them overdue by themselves each day. Returning a book late charges a fine per day, and a book shows as on loan once every copy is out. Home shows titles, loans out, overdue loans and fines charged; reports show loans by category, overdue loans and the most borrowed titles.
- **Nursery & daycare** places each child in a room by age, keeps allergies in view on the child's page and the home page, and lets a child move room when they are old enough. An incident is closed only after the parent has been told what happened and what was done. Home shows children with allergies, those ready to move room and open incidents; reports show children by room and incidents by month.
- **Driving school** books lessons only for active learners who still have lessons left in their package, never two in the same car or with the same instructor at the same time, and counts lessons bought, completed, missed and remaining on the learner. A test is booked only with a learner's licence, and a learner is licensed only after a test was booked. Home shows today's lessons and packages used up; reports show lessons by instructor, vehicle use and learners by licence code.
- **Learning management (LMS)** publishes a course only once it has a published lesson, numbers lessons uniquely within their course, enrols learners only on published courses at the course price, and moves an enrolment from not started to in progress to completed as progress is updated. Each course shows its learners, completion rate and revenue. Home shows published courses, learners learning now and this month's enrolments; reports show courses, enrolments by month and top courses.
- **Exams & report cards** keep marks between zero and the exam's maximum, one per student per exam, and give each a letter grade. Marks are entered while an exam is marking and locked once it is published. A report card compiles the student's average and class position from the published exams of its term and class, and is issued only with a teacher's comment. Home shows marking in progress and report cards to issue; reports show results by subject and report cards by class.
- **Timetabling** refuses a period that ends before it starts and never puts a class, teacher or room in two active periods at once on the same day. Each period knows its length, and the week's load is totted up by teacher and class. Home shows today's periods and periods without a teacher; reports show periods by day, teacher load and class hours.
