title: Airport transfers, parking and toll, driving school and GPS tracking apps get real rules
---

- **Airport shuttles & chauffeur services**: an airport pick-up needs the flight number. The name board shows the passenger's name unless you give other text. The party must fit the vehicle class, and a driver can't take two transfers less than two hours apart. The home page lists today's transfers in pick-up order, and the reports show fares by transfer type and by driver.
- **Parking lots & toll plazas**: shifts can only be opened at open sites, and a cashier can't have two shifts open at once. Closing a shift adds up the cash and card takings and compares the total with the expected takings. The shift is marked short or over when the two don't match, and the difference is kept. Each site shows this month's takings, and the reports show takings by site and differences by cashier.
- **Driving school**: a lesson can only be booked for a learner who has lessons left on their package and whose learner's licence is still valid. An instructor can't teach two lessons within the same hour. Completing a lesson uses up one of the learner's lessons, and booked lessons from earlier days are marked missed each morning. A learner can have only one test booked at a time. Passing the test marks the learner as passed, and failing sends them back to learning. The report shows the pass rate at each testing centre.
- **GPS tracking & trip log**: a tracker's IMEI must have 15 digits and can't be used on two trackers. Removed trackers can't log trips or alerts. A trip over 120 km/h raises a speeding alert. An alert is acknowledged before it is closed, and panic alerts are listed first. Each morning, trackers whose subscription has run out are marked offline. Each tracker shows its trips, distance and top speed for the last 30 days.
