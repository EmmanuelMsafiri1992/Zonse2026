title: Fleet, minibus collections, maintenance, visitors and compliance get real rules
---

- **Fleet & vehicles**:
  - Registrations are unique however they are typed. Odometers only go forward, so a fuel slip can't read less than the vehicle has already done.
  - Each fill works out the distance since the last one and the kilometres per litre. Reports show fuel cost per kilometre by vehicle.
  - Finishing a service books the next one six months or 15,000 km on.
  - Each morning vehicles with an expired licence disc or insurance come off the road until it is renewed. The home page lists services, licences and insurance coming due.
- **Minibus daily collections**:
  - Each vehicle cashes up once a day. The driver owes the day's target less the fuel they paid for, and any cash under that marks the collection short.
  - A shortfall paid later clears the collection once it is covered. The home page adds up what each driver still owes.
- **Equipment maintenance**:
  - Serial numbers are unique, and retired equipment takes no new work.
  - A breakdown marks the equipment faulty until it is fixed. Finishing preventive work sets the next service date from the service interval.
  - Each morning a work order is raised for every service due within a week. Done and cancelled work orders stay closed.
- **Visitor management**:
  - Visitors sign in with their arrival time, and the same ID number can't be signed in twice at once. Signing out records how long they stayed.
  - The home page shows who is on site for an evacuation roll call. Visits left open from earlier days are closed each morning and flagged.
- **Compliance & audits**:
  - An obligation's status follows its expiry date: compliant, due soon within 30 days, or lapsed. It is brought up to date each morning. Renewing records the new expiry, licence number and fee.
  - Findings get a fix-by date from their severity (7 days for critical, up to 90 for low) and close only once the corrective action is written down.
