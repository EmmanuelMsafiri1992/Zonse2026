title: Live chat, loyalty, feedback, contracts, affiliate, proposals and commissions apps get real rules
---

- **Live chat**: each keyword can belong to only one active bot reply. When a new chat matches a keyword, the bot answers it in the transcript and the chat waits on the customer. Replies set to hand over leave the chat open for a person to pick up. Chats left waiting for 7 quiet days resolve by themselves. The home page shows open chats nobody is handling, and the report shows chats by channel.
- **Loyalty & rewards**: members earn one point for every 10 spent unless you give the points yourself. Inactive members can't earn, and nobody can redeem more points than they have. The tier follows lifetime points: bronze, then silver at 500, gold at 2,000 and platinum at 5,000. Earned or redeemed points can be reversed. The home page shows the top members, and the report shows points by month.
- **Customer feedback & NPS**: scores run from 0 to 10, and only live surveys take responses. Each survey shows its NPS, with promoters, passives and detractors. Surveys close by themselves after their end date. Unhappy customers appear on the home page until you save a reply to them. The reports show NPS by month and feedback by source.
- **Contracts & e-signature**: a contract must end after it starts. Signing it needs the signatory and a signing date that isn't in the future, and a signed contract becomes active once it starts. At its end date, an active contract renews for the same term if it auto-renews, and expires if it doesn't. Pending obligations past their due date are marked missed. The home page shows contracts ending in the next 60 days.
- **Affiliate & referral management**: referral codes are stored in capitals and each code belongs to one affiliate. Commission rates run from 0 to 50%. Paused affiliates can't take new referrals. A converted referral needs its sale value and earns the affiliate's rate at the moment it converted. You can pay referrals one at a time, or pay everything an affiliate is owed at once.
- **Proposals & sales documents**: a proposal must have a price before it is sent. Sending it keeps it valid for 30 days, and sent proposals past that date expire each night and can't be accepted. A template can start a new draft proposal with its text. The home page shows proposals that expire within 7 days and your win rate.
- **Sales commissions & targets**: commission is the sale value times the rate, which is 5% unless you set another. Each target tracks the rep's sales in its period. It is marked achieved when sales reach the target, and missed if the period ends short. Commissions move from earned to approved to paid, and the report shows commissions by rep.
