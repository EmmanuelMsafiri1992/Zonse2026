title: Personal apps get real rules
---

- **Personal finance**: income can only go under income categories, and spending only under spending categories. A savings goal shows how much to save each month to reach the target date, and it is marked reached once the target is met. Making a debt payment lowers the balance and moves the next payment on a month. The home page shows this month's income, spending and what is left over.
- **Personal tasks & habits**: ticking off a habit adds to its streak if it was kept up on time, and starts again at one if not. The best streak is kept. Each night, missed streaks go back to zero.
- **Family & household**: when a repeating chore is done, the next one is added for the same person, and its pocket-money reward is earned. Family events that have passed are marked done. The shopping list is grouped by shop, and buying an item records its price.
- **Freelancer toolkit**: hourly, daily and fixed gigs each work out their own total. Logging hours updates the total. A gig can only be marked paid once the invoice has gone out. Each gig shows what it earned per hour, and the reports compare earnings with expenses.
- **CV & portfolio**: an experience entry becomes past once its end date passes. A job application gets a follow-up date a week after applying, then moves on to interview, offer and accepted. A portfolio piece needs a link or a description before it can be published.
- **My wedding / event planner**: only guests who are attending count towards the headcount. A deposit can't be more than the cost. Paying a budget line shows how far it went over or under the estimate.
- **Health & fitness tracker**: blood pressure is checked and flagged when it is high. Each check-in shows the weight change since the last one. Active minutes are counted against a weekly goal of 150, and medicines that need a refill are listed.
- **Small landlord**: rent received is graded as paid, part paid, late or missed. An open repair marks the unit as being repaired. The home page shows rent that hasn't come in yet and leases that end soon.
