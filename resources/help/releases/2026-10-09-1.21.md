title: Canteen, scholarships, training centres, discipline and sports academies get real rules
---

- **Canteen** works a cashless card's balance out from its top-ups less its card sales. A card sale needs an active card with enough money on it and stays within the card's daily limit, and a refund or reversed top-up puts the balance right. Card numbers are unique, blocked and lost cards cannot spend, and cards running low are flagged. Home shows today's sales and low balances; reports show sales by month, the cards on issue and top-ups by method.
- **Scholarships** takes applications only for open schemes before their deadline, one per applicant per scheme, and never awards more places or money than the scheme has. An award is disbursed only after it is made, and each scheme shows what has been awarded, paid out and is left.
- **Training centres & vocational colleges** enrols trainees only on open or running intakes with a free seat, never takes more than the course fee, and declares a trainee competent or not yet competent only with an assessment result. An intake starts running on its start date, completes only once every trainee is assessed or has dropped out, and shows its seats, fees outstanding, attendance and pass rate.
- **Discipline & behaviour tracking** adds points for merits and commendations, takes them away for demerits, detentions and suspensions, and keeps each student's running total. Serious entries need a parent on record before they can be marked as parent informed, nothing is logged for a future date, and students who fall below the at-risk line are flagged on the home page.
- **Sports academies & coaching** puts athletes only in active squads that suit their age group, holds sessions only for active squads once the day has come, and never records more attendance than the squad has athletes. Each squad tracks its athletes, injuries, sessions, attendance and monthly fees, and a squad with athletes cannot be switched off.
