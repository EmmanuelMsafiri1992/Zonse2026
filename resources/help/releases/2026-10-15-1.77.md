---

Until now a Zonseob plan was confirmed by hand after you got in touch. You can now pay for it yourself from **Settings → Plans & billing**:

- **Paynow** takes EcoCash, OneMoney, InnBucks, ZimSwitch and Visa or Mastercard. Where Zonseob's Paynow account is in Zimbabwe Gold, you are charged the local equivalent of the dollar price at the day's rate, which is shown before you pay.
- **Stripe** takes international cards in US dollars.
- **Bank transfer or mobile money** to Zonseob's own account, for people who would rather not pay online. Tell us the reference you used and our team confirms it within one working day.

The dollar price of every plan is shown alongside its local-currency equivalent, so you know what will leave your wallet.

Paying for the plan you are already on adds a month or a year from the end of your current period, so paying early never loses you days. Paying for a different plan starts it today.

## Trials, grace and paused workspaces

A free trial still lasts fourteen days. Seven days before it ends a reminder appears at the top of every page. When a trial or a paid period runs out you get a further seven days of grace with everything still working; after that the workspace is paused until a plan is paid. Nothing is deleted while paused: owners can still reach billing, everyone can reach their profile, and the apps open again the moment a payment is confirmed.

Workspaces on the Free plan are never paused.

## For the people who run Zonseob

A new **Platform payments** page (in the settings menu for super admins) lists every payment, lets you confirm or reject bank transfers, and holds the exchange rates behind local prices and the bank-transfer instructions people see.

Choosing a plan on the public pricing page now carries that choice through sign-up: the plan you clicked is recommended when you reach the plan step.
