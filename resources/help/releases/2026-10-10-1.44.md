title: Property apps get real rules
---

- **Maintenance requests**: the fix-by date follows the urgency: urgent the next day, normal within a week, low within two weeks. A request is assigned only to a named contractor or a staff member. Finishing it records how many days the fix took. The home page lists requests past their fix-by date, and the report shows repair speed and cost by category.
- **Property listings**: a listing goes on the market only with an asking price, and a sole mandate needs an expiry date. A rental is let and a sale is sold, never the other way round. Viewings are booked only on listings that are on the market, one prospect per time slot. Booked viewings are cancelled when the listing is sold, let or withdrawn. The home page shows today's viewings and mandates about to expire.
- **Rent collection**: a charge is the rent plus utility recharges, and each unit is charged once per period. Payments received add up on the charge, and a payment can't be more than what is still owed. A charge turns part paid or paid, or goes into arrears once it is past its due date (checked each night).
- **Estates, HOAs & levies**: participation quotas can't add up to more than 100%. An ordinary levy defaults to the owner's monthly levy and is due in 30 days, and each owner is billed for each type once per period. An owner's balance and arrears status follow their unpaid levies. A matter is resolved only with a written resolution.
- **Short-stay rental**: stays can't overlap at the same listing, check-out must be after check-in, and blocked listings can't take bookings. The payout defaults to the nights times the nightly rate plus the cleaning fee. The next guest can't check in until the turnover clean after the last guest is done. The home page shows today's arrivals, departures and cleans, and the report shows occupancy per listing.
- **Land & plot sales**: a plot is sold to one buyer at a time, and the sale price defaults to the plot price. Payments can't be more than the price. A sale moves from reserved to paying to fully paid as instalments come in, and the plot follows: reserved, sold, transferred, or available again if the sale is cancelled. Transfer needs full payment and a title deed number.
- **Mortgage / home-loan origination**: the loan defaults to the purchase price less the deposit and can't be more than that. Each application shows the monthly repayment, the loan-to-value and the share of income. An application can't be submitted without a rate and term, and it is approved only when the repayment is at most 30% of income. It moves forward one stage at a time.
