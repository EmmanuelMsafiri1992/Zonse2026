title: Approval rules for app records
---

Approval rules now cover more than invoices and quotes. A rule can say that marking a record "approved" needs a yes first. This works in every app that has an "approved" status, such as bills, expense claims, leave requests and payroll runs.

- **Set it up** in Settings → Approval rules. Pick "Approving a bill (Payables & bills)" or another step, choose a starting amount, and choose who approves. Only the apps you have switched on are listed.
- **On the record**, someone who can't approve sees a notice and an "Ask for approval" button. Until an approver says yes, they can't move the record to approved, whether from the form, the status buttons or the API.
- **Approvers**, and the workspace owner, can mark records approved straight away. Doing that also settles any request still waiting for that record.
- Records below the amount, and records added by imports or behind the scenes, are not held.
