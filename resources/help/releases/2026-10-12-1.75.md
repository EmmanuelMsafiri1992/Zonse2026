title: Approval rules cover lists that start as approved
---

Some lists start new entries as approved, such as Suppliers and the brand asset library. A new entry left on that starting status now goes through your approval rules the same way as one marked approved by hand.

- If a rule covers it, someone who can't approve gets a message to save it with another status (for example Pending) and ask for approval from its page.
- Owners, and the approvers named on the rule, can still add entries straight away.
