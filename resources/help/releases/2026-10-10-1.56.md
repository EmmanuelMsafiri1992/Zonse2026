title: Utility billing, ISP billing, solar PAYG and refuse collection apps get real rules
---

- **Water & electricity billing**: a meter number can be registered only once, and readings can only be taken on active meters. A reading starts from the meter's last reading and can't go below it. Post-paid meters are billed for the units used at the rate in their tariff, with the bill due 14 days later. A reading more than three times the recent average is flagged as high usage. A disputed bill can be re-issued with a corrected reading, and the home page lists the meters not yet read this month.
- **ISP / WISP billing**: an active subscriber needs a PPPoE username, and a username or IP address can't be used by two subscribers. Activating a subscriber pays them up for the first month, and a payment moves their paid-until date on by the months paid. Subscribers more than 3 days overdue are suspended each morning. Faults can't be logged for cancelled subscribers, a technician is named before one is sent, and the report shows how long each kind of fault took to fix.
- **Solar energy & PAYG systems**: a controller serial can be used on only one system, and a pay-as-you-go system needs a daily rate. A payment reduces the balance, buys days of power and issues a new unlock code. Paying off the balance unlocks the system for good. Systems past their paid-until date are locked each morning and can be repossessed after 30 days locked. Readings with battery health under 60% raise an alert.
- **Waste management & refuse collection**: an active collection point needs a bin, a collection day and a route. Each morning a run is scheduled for every route with collections that day, and runs left scheduled from earlier days are marked missed. Completing a run records the stops done and the tonnage, and tonnage needs the landfill or weighbridge ticket. The report shows tonnage by route.
