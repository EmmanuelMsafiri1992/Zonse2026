---
version: 1.69
date: 2026-10-12
title: Worked-out figures stay worked out everywhere
---

Release 1.68 stopped figures the app works out, such as a bill's amount paid, from being typed over on the app's own forms. The other ways records come in now leave them alone too:

- **Data import**: these columns are no longer in the import template. If a file has them, they are ignored and the app works the figures out as each row is saved. A loyalty member's opening points can still be imported for a new member.
- **Public web forms**: these fields can no longer be added to a form. Forms that already had one leave it out.
- **Phone (USSD) logging**: callers are no longer asked for a figure the app works out, such as a bar tab's total. The app works it out from what they entered.
