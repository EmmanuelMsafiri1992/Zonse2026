title: HR, attendance, rosters, performance, training, staff requests and disciplinary cases get real rules
---

- **Employees (HR)**:
  - Employee numbers and ID numbers are unique however they are typed. A fixed-term contract needs an end date.
  - Signing a contract carries its salary and employment type onto the employee and closes the contract it replaces.
  - Each morning expired documents and contracts are marked. The home page shows headcount and what runs out within 30 days.
- **Attendance & timesheets**:
  - Each employee clocks in once a day, and clocking in more than 10 minutes after 08:00 marks them late. Clocking out records the hours on site.
  - A timesheet covers one Monday-to-Sunday week per employee. It can be filled from that week's clock-ins, with anything over 40 hours counted as overtime, then submitted and approved.
- **Shifts & rosters**:
  - Nobody can be rostered on two overlapping shifts, and night shifts that run past midnight are understood.
  - Approving a swap moves the shift to the new person, but only if they are free. Reports total the hours each person was rostered and worked.
- **Performance & OKRs**:
  - Reviews move from self review to manager review to completed, and can't be completed without a rating. Nobody reviews themselves or gives 360 feedback about themselves.
  - An objective's status follows its progress and due date: achieved at 100%, behind once overdue, at risk when due within two weeks and under 75%.
- **Training & certifications**:
  - Certificate numbers are unique. A certification shows as expiring within 60 days of its expiry and expired after it, refreshed each morning.
  - Courses show their cost per certificate, and reports show training spend by type.
- **Employee self-service portal**:
  - Staff requests are picked up and closed only with a response to the employee, and reports show how long each type takes.
  - Publishing an announcement dates it and puts it on the home page.
- **Disciplinary & grievance cases**:
  - A hearing needs a date, and a case can't close without an outcome. Grievances are upheld or not upheld, and disciplinary cases end in a sanction.
  - Warnings stay on file for 3, 6 or 12 months, and each case shows the employee's live warnings.
