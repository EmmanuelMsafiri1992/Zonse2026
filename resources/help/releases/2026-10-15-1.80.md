title: A calmer, clearer look across Zonseob
---

Zonseob now looks like one product from the public pages to the apps inside.

**Public pages.** The home page opens on a light screen with a picture of the real dashboard, one "Start free" button and the facts that matter: how many apps, multi-branch, pay in your own currency. **Zonseob AI** sits right under it with a sample conversation and the token packs priced in US dollars and your local currency. Suites are a compact list, professions a plain list, and pricing shows one highlighted plan. The navigation uses the same small mark and name as the app, so the brand is the same everywhere.

**Zonseob AI** is now a full-height chat: your chats on the left, the conversation in the middle, and the question box pinned to the bottom. Your token balance sits in the bar at the top, and **Buy tokens** opens the packs and payment choices in a window instead of taking up the page. Links to `#buy-tokens` open that window straight away.

**Dashboard.** The trial reminder now lives only in the bar at the top and in the Plan tile, so the page no longer repeats it. Your first four ready apps are shown large with a line about what they do, the rest as a compact row, and the "At a glance" summaries for your most recently used apps sit below them. The welcome tour shows once and does not come back unless you start it from Help.

**Everywhere.** Slightly larger text, more breathing room between sections, status labels drawn as tinted outlines instead of filled pills, and cards inside cards shown flat.
