title: Tested restores and a workspace privacy check
---

Behind-the-scenes work that keeps your data safe.

- **Backups can now be restored in one step.** Nightly backups were already taken. A new restore command puts one back, and it saves a copy of the current data first so the restore itself can be undone. Restores are now tested along with the backups.
- **Your data stays in your workspace.** A new automatic check tries to open, change and delete another workspace's contacts, invoices, records, signatures, settings and more by changing the numbers in links and forms. It runs with every update. Every attempt is refused, and nothing in the other workspace changes.
