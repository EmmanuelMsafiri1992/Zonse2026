title: Worked-out figures can't be typed over
---

Figures an app works out for itself now appear on the form as a grey box marked "Worked out by the app", with no input. Before, you could type into them, and the app then quietly replaced what you typed when you saved. On a new record they read "Worked out once saved".

This covers 50 fields in 43 apps, for example:

- **Payables**: a bill's amount paid.
- **Payroll**: a pay run's totals and employee count, and a payslip's net pay.
- **Budgeting**: a budget's total.
- **Rent collection**: a charge's total and amount paid.
- **Hostel**: a room's occupied beds.
- **Ticketing and cinema**: seats and tickets sold.
- **Weighbridge**: net weight.
- **Fuel station**: litres sold on a shift.
- **Utility billing**: a reading's consumption.
- **Time tracking and legal**: an entry's billable amount.
- **Exams**: a mark's grade.

**Loyalty**: you can still type a new member's opening points. After that, their balance comes from their transactions.

Some figures are only worked out when certain details are filled in, such as a produce sale's price per kg or a tax return's output and input tax. These stay editable, so you can still type them yourself when those details are left blank.
