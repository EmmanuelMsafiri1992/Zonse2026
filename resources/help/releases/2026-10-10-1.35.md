title: Stock, warehouses, suppliers, production, orders, couriers and freight get real rules
---

- **Inventory & stock**:
  - SKUs and barcodes are unique. Stock on hand changes only through movements, receipts or stock counts, so every change is recorded. Stock can't go below zero, and discontinued items can't be received.
  - Posted movements are locked and can be reversed with a reason. A stock count posts the difference as an adjustment.
  - Batches expire on their expiry date and can be recalled with a reason. The home page shows items to reorder and batches expiring soon.
  - Reports show stock valuation, movements by type, and shrinkage and damage.
- **Warehouse management**:
  - Bin codes are unique within a warehouse, and a bin shows in use or empty from its contents. Blocking a bin needs a reason.
  - Pick lists only move forward, and packing needs a package count. Transfers must go to a different warehouse.
  - A warehouse can't close while bins hold stock or picks and transfers are open, and a closed warehouse takes no new work.
- **Suppliers & vendors**:
  - Approval needs a tax number and bank details, and tax numbers are unique. Blocking needs a reason and withdraws the supplier's prices.
  - A supplier keeps one current price per item: a new price replaces the old one. Prices past their valid-until date expire each day.
  - The price comparison shows the cheapest current supplier for each item and the saving against the dearest.
- **Manufacturing & production**:
  - A bill of materials reads lines like "4 x Leg @ 5", and its unit cost is worked out from them. A bill can't be retired while open work orders use it.
  - Work orders list the materials they need, only move forward, can't make more than ordered, and are costed with scrap included.
  - A quality check passes at up to 2.5% defects. A failed check sends the work order back to production, and a work order is completed only after a passed check.
- **Order management**:
  - An order reads lines like "2 x Widget @ 10", and its total follows them. Orders only move forward and can be cancelled until they ship.
  - Items can be back-ordered from the order. While back-orders wait, the order can only be part shipped, and it becomes shipped when the last one is fulfilled. Cancelling an order cancels its back-orders.
  - Reports show orders by channel, on-time shipping and back-ordered items.
- **Delivery & courier**:
  - Every parcel gets a unique tracking number. Delivery needs the receiver's name, and cash-on-delivery parcels need the full amount collected.
  - A failed attempt needs a reason, and after three failed attempts the parcel goes back to the sender.
  - A driver has one run a day. Starting it sends their parcels out, and it can't be completed while any are still out.
  - Reports show the success rate by driver and the cash on delivery.
- **Freight forwarding**:
  - A shipment needs different origin and destination, an ETA after departure, and a bill of lading once it moves.
  - Container numbers are checked with their ISO 6346 check digit. Containers belong to sea shipments and need a seal once loaded.
  - A customs entry goes lodged, assessed, paid and released, and a query sends it back to lodged. Releasing the entry releases the shipment.
  - Containers kept past their return-by date run up demurrage each day, shown on the home page and in reports.
