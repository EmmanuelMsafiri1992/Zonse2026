title: Mosques, fundraising, elections, cinemas and sports leagues get real rules
---

- **Mosque & madrasa** records contributions with an amount and a type, receipts each one once with a numbered receipt, and keeps zakat apart from sadaqah and the other funds. Madrasa students carry a monthly fee and their hifz progress. Home shows this month's giving, this year's zakat and the madrasa roll; reports show contributions by month, type and method, and the madrasa by class.
- **Fundraising & donations** works out what each campaign has raised from donations received, with pledges counted separately against the goal. Donations go only to live campaigns, are receipted once received, and get a tax certificate only once receipted. A campaign closes by itself when its end date passes. Reports show donations by month and by method.
- **Voting, elections & online polls** moves an election from draft to nominations, voting, closed and results published. Candidates are nominated only while nominations are open. Each election has one voter register with unique member numbers and voting codes, and each voter votes once while voting is open. Votes never exceed the voters who turned out, voting closes by itself at the closing date, and publishing results elects the leading candidate for each position. Reports show results and turnout by method.
- **Cinema & theatre booking** books seats only for shows on sale, never the same seat twice and never beyond the auditorium. A show counts its seats sold and switches to sold out and back as bookings come and go. A booking is paid before its tickets are collected, and a show is completed only once it has screened. Reports show bookings by month and takings by screen.
- **Sports leagues & clubs** sets fixtures only between two different active teams, never with either team playing twice on the same day, and a fixture counts as played only once both scores are in. Each team keeps its played, won, drawn, lost, goals and points, and the reports show the league table. Each player has one jersey number per team and moves between teams by transfer, and a team cannot withdraw with fixtures still to play.
