title: Webhooks and automations for every app
---

Records in every app you have switched on now set off webhooks and automations, not just contacts, tasks, tickets, invoices, payments and appointments.

- **Two new events:** `record.created` when a record is added in any app, and `record.updated` when one is changed. The webhook body is the same as the record from the API, with the app's own values under `fields`.
- **Automations** can test the app, the list, the title, the status, the amount and who it is assigned to. For example: "when a bill over 1,000 is added in Payables & bills, create an approval task for the person it is assigned to".
- A "was just changed" condition also works on an app's own fields.
- Changes the app makes behind the scenes, such as marking a sale as billed, don't count as changes.
