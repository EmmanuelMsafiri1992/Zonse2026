title: More property apps get real rules
---

- **Property valuation**: a report is due a week after instruction unless a date is set. The inspection date can't be in the future, and a valuation is issued only with a market value and a report link. Each valuation shows its value per m². The home page lists overdue reports, and the report shows turnaround and fees by purpose.
- **Facility management & cleaning schedules**: checks are logged only for open areas and never for a future date, and a check that found a problem says what it was. Each area remembers its last clean and shows as overdue once its frequency has passed. An issue is closed by writing down the fix. Reports show checks by area and by cleaner.
- **Co-working & desk booking**: a desk or room can't be double-booked, a booking must end after it starts, and only active members can book. Members can be paused, resumed or cancelled, and cancelling a member cancels their upcoming bookings. The home page shows today's bookings and monthly membership fees.
- **Self-storage units**: a unit is rented to one customer at a time, and each rental gets its own gate code (one is made up when none is given). Rent defaults to the unit rate and is paid a month ahead. A rental past its paid-until date goes into arrears each night and the unit is overlocked. Paying clears it, and moving out frees the unit and cancels the code.
- **Parking management**: registrations are stored in capitals without spaces, so the same car always matches. A car holds one live permit at a time and can't be parked twice at once. Permit holders park free, and visitors pay before or after leaving. Each night expired permits are closed and cars left parked from earlier days are flagged unpaid.
- **Tenant portal**: an account goes live only with an email or phone number, and an email belongs to one account. Disabled accounts can't send new requests. A request is closed only with a reply, and it records how many days the answer took. The home page lists requests waiting for an answer.
