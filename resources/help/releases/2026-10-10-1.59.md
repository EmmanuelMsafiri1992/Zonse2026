title: Reports fixes for library, rehabilitation and performance apps
---

- **Library**: the reports page no longer fails when a loan has no book or its book was deleted. Those loans are counted under "Uncategorised" and "Unknown".
- **Rehabilitation & occupational therapy**: the reports page no longer fails when a session has no case. Those sessions are counted under "Private".
- **Performance & OKRs**: the reports page now opens again for completed reviews.
