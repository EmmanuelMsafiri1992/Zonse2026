title: Pages open instantly
---

- **No more waiting between pages**: clicking a menu item, link or button now swaps in the new page without reloading the whole screen. The menu, top bar and your place in the sidebar stay put.
- **Ready before you click**: the pages in your menu load quietly in the background, and a link starts loading as soon as you rest the pointer on it (or touch it on a phone), so most pages appear the moment you click. A page that isn't ready yet shows its outline straight away while it finishes.
- **Forms too**: saving a form shows the result in place. If something needs fixing, the page stays put, keeps what you typed and shows what to fix.
- **A steady address bar**: the address bar keeps the address you opened Zonseo on instead of changing with every page. Back, Forward and refresh still take you to the page you were on. To share or bookmark a particular page, open it in a new tab.
- **Back is instant**: the Back button shows the page you came from straight away and refreshes it quietly.
- Downloads, signing out and switching workspace still load the whole page.
