title: Permits, risk registers, safety incidents, citizen desks, council rates and courts get real rules
---

- **Permits & licences**:
  - An application comes in with a decision due within a month. It goes under review and is inspected when its type needs one.
  - It is approved only after an inspection has passed.
  - An issued licence gets a unique number and an expiry date.
  - Home shows decisions that are overdue. Reports show applications by type and by month, and inspections by outcome.
- **Risk register**:
  - Each risk is scored as likelihood times impact on a five-by-five grid and rated low, medium, high or critical.
  - A risk counts as mitigated only when it has a mitigation plan, and a critical risk cannot simply be accepted.
  - Each review re-scores the risk and sets the next review date.
  - Home shows the top risks, a heat map and reviews that are overdue. Reports show risks by category and by rating.
- **Incident reporting (H&S)**:
  - Serious and fatal incidents must be reported to the authority, and an investigation ends with a root cause.
  - Corrective actions are raised against the incident, done and then verified.
  - An incident closes only once its root cause is recorded and none of its actions is still open.
  - Reports show incidents by month, severity and type.
- **Citizen service desk & complaints**:
  - Each request gets a service-level due date from its type and is routed to a department.
  - A request is resolved with a written resolution, and it escalates by itself once it passes its due date unresolved.
  - Reports show requests by department, type, ward and month.
- **Council revenue (rates)**:
  - A rates bill adds up its rates, refuse, water and sewer charges. It goes only to active properties that are not exempt.
  - A bill takes payments up to its balance and turns overdue by itself after its due date.
  - A property's balance is the sum of its unpaid bills, and the property is marked in arrears while it has an overdue bill.
  - A property can be handed over for collection only while it is in arrears.
  - Home lists the largest arrears. Reports show billing by month and properties by category.
- **Court case management & cause lists**:
  - Each case file has one case number.
  - Hearings are set down only on open cases, and never two in the same courtroom at the same time.
  - A hearing is heard or postponed only once its day has come. When it is heard, the case becomes part heard. When it is postponed, the next hearing is set down on the new date.
  - Reports show cases by court and type, and hearings by courtroom and month.
