title: Consignment stock, equipment hire, weighbridges, quality and asset tracking get real rules
---

- **Wholesale distribution & consignment stock**:
  - A consignment reads lines like "20 x Cement @ 15", and its stock value follows them.
  - Settlements can't add up to more units than were placed, and the amount due is their value less the dealer's commission. The consignment's sold value and status follow its settlements, and it is settled once everything is sold and paid.
  - Taking back unsold stock needs the count returned. Reports show sell-through by dealer and settlements by month.
- **Equipment & tool hire**:
  - Hire item names are unique, and a booking reads lines like "100 x Chairs". A booking can't take more than is free on its dates, and items in maintenance or retired can't be booked.
  - The hire total is the daily rate times the days out, counting both ends. Bookings only move forward.
  - Late returns are charged at the daily rate, and damage charges need a description. The deposit kept, the refund and anything still owed are worked out on return.
- **Weighbridge & bulk dispatch**:
  - A truck has one open ticket at a time, however its registration is typed.
  - The second weigh completes the ticket: the heavier mass is the gross, the lighter the tare, and the net is worked out. A second weigh that goes the wrong way for the direction is refused.
  - Completed tickets are locked, voiding needs a reason, loads over 56 t gross are flagged, and outbound tickets print a dispatch note.
- **Quality management**:
  - Major and critical non-conformances need an owner. A non-conformance closes only with a root cause and at least one corrective action, all of them verified.
  - Corrective actions need a due date, only move forward, and are verified with an effectiveness check before they close.
  - An audit is done once its findings are written, and a finding can raise a non-conformance in one step. Reports show non-conformances by source and the on-time rate for corrective actions.
- **Asset tracking**:
  - Every tag is unique, however it is typed. Only an available asset can be checked out, and the due date can't be before the check-out.
  - Returning an asset records its condition. A damaged asset goes into repair, and a lost or disposed asset stays out of circulation.
  - Check-outs past their due date turn overdue each morning. Reports show check-outs by holder and assets by status.
