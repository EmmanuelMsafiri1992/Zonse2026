title: Mines, weighbridges, factories, labs, permits and contractor access get real rules
---

- **Mine & quarry operations**:
  - Each pit gets one report per shift per day. Figures can't be negative, downtime can't exceed the 12-hour shift, and approved reports are locked. Tonnes per load and availability are worked out.
  - A blast can't be fired without its holes and explosives, or while a misfire is still to be cleared. The home page warns about open misfires, and vibration over 12.5 mm/s is flagged.
  - Stockpiles are drawn down and surveyed, never go below zero, and are marked depleted when empty.
  - Reports show production by pit, tonnes by month and the blasting register.
- **Weighbridge**:
  - The second weigh completes a ticket in either direction: the heavier mass is the gross and the lighter is the tare, so the net is never typed. A vehicle has one open ticket at a time.
  - A vehicle's stored tare from the last 30 days can be used instead of a second weigh. Loads over 56 t are flagged as overloads.
  - Completed tickets can't have their masses changed, and voiding needs a reason.
  - Reports show tonnage by product, overloads and voided tickets.
- **Shop-floor execution (MES)**:
  - A machine runs one job at a time, can't start a job while down or in maintenance, and its status follows its runs. A breakdown stops the running job.
  - Downtime needs a reason. When a run ends, its availability, performance, quality and OEE are worked out.
  - Reports show OEE by machine, downtime reasons and scrap by product.
- **Lab testing & certificates of analysis**:
  - Results pass or fail automatically against specifications like "≤ 10", "6.5 - 8.5", "min 5" or "Absent". Samples move to testing and then to results ready as results come in.
  - A certificate needs every result decided. It is numbered, printable, and locks the results.
  - Rejecting a sample needs a reason. Results are due five days after receipt, and overdue samples show on the home page.
  - Reports show turnaround and pass rate by matrix.
- **Permit to work**:
  - Hot work and confined spaces need a gas test before work starts. Confined spaces and electrical work need their isolations listed, and a permit must end after it starts.
  - Nobody issues a permit to themselves, and a location holds one live permit at a time.
  - Permits only move forward. Suspending or cancelling needs a reason, and resuming hot work needs a fresh gas test. Permits left active overnight are suspended.
- **Contractor & site access**:
  - Inductions last 12 months. Only inducted workers with a valid induction and medical can sign in, and nobody can be signed in twice. Hours on site are worked out at sign-out.
  - Blocking a worker needs a reason and signs them out. Lapsed inductions and medicals expire workers each day.
  - The home page shows who is on site and what expires within 30 days. Reports show hours by company.
