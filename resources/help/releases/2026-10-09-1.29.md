title: Pharmacy, hospital wards, telemedicine, specialist practices, radiology, blood banks and ambulances get real rules
---

- **Pharmacy**:
  - Stock goes up with what is received and down with what is dispensed. Nothing is dispensed beyond what is in stock, from expired stock or for a discontinued drug.
  - Prescription-only drugs need the prescription details.
  - The controlled-drugs register keeps a running balance for each controlled drug. Destroying a controlled drug needs a witness, and the witness must be someone else.
  - Register entries can be verified by another member of the team.
- **Hospital & wards**:
  - Each ward shows its occupied beds, free beds and occupancy. A full ward cannot take another admission, and a bed holds one patient at a time.
  - Patients can be transferred between wards, discharged with a discharge summary, transferred out or recorded as deceased.
  - Theatre bookings are only for patients in hospital, never before the admission, and never with the surgeon already in another operation at the same time.
- **Telemedicine**:
  - Each consult gets its own video meeting link. Consults are booked for today or later, and a doctor cannot be double-booked.
  - The doctor admits the patient, joins and ends the call with consultation notes. The call length is recorded.
  - E-prescriptions are issued only during or after the call. They are sent to a named pharmacy and then dispensed or cancelled.
  - Consults that were never started become no-shows the next day.
- **Dental, optometry & physio**:
  - Procedures are booked only on treatment plans the patient has accepted.
  - Dental procedures use FDI tooth numbers, such as 11 or 36.
  - Each plan tracks procedures done and scheduled, the amount charged against its estimate, and its progress. It completes only once no procedure is still scheduled.
- **Radiology & diagnostic imaging (RIS)**:
  - A study moves from booked to scanned to reported, and a reported study cannot go back.
  - Reporting needs the radiologist's report. The report time is tracked, and reports taking more than 48 hours are flagged as late.
- **Blood bank**:
  - Donors give blood at least 56 days apart, and a unit must match the donor's blood group.
  - Units stay in quarantine until they screen negative. Reactive units are discarded and the donor is deferred.
  - Shelf life is set by component: 35 days for whole blood, 42 for red cells, 365 for plasma and 5 for platelets. Expired units are taken out of stock automatically.
  - Units are issued first-expiry-first-out.
- **Ambulance & emergency dispatch**:
  - An ambulance can be on only one call at a time.
  - Each call records the time of every stage and the response time, measured against targets of 15, 30 and 60 minutes for priorities 1 to 3.
  - A patient taken to hospital needs the hospital named, and a call closes only with a completed patient care report. A call never goes back a stage.
