title: Every app's records through the API
---

The API now reaches the records of every app you have switched on, not just contacts, tasks, tickets, invoices and appointments. Each list has its own address, for example `/api/v1/apps/clinic/patients` or `/api/v1/apps/payables/bills`.

- **Read keys** can list and open records. Lists take `q` to search, `status`, `contact_id` and `assignee_id`, and are paged like the other lists.
- **Write keys** can also add, change and delete records. A change only needs the values that are different.
- The app's own fields go in `fields`, using the same names as the app's CSV export.
- The same rules apply as on the app's forms. For example, a supplier invoice can't be captured twice.
- Figures the app works out itself are listed in `worked_out` and are ignored if sent.

Apps that are switched off answer with "forbidden". The endpoint list is under **Settings > API & webhooks**.
