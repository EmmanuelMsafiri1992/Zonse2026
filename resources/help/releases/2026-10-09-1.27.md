title: Grants, data privacy, environmental permits, parliament and prisons get real rules
---

- **Grants & subsidies management**:
  - Applications are taken only while a programme is open, one per applicant per programme.
  - An award is never more than what was asked for or what is left in the programme's budget.
  - Disbursed grants owe a report by their report date, and home lists the reports that are due.
  - A programme closes by itself after its closing date. It completes only once every grant on it is settled.
  - Reports show applications by status and by month.
- **Data privacy (GDPR / POPIA)**:
  - Every active processing activity states how long the data is kept.
  - A data-subject request is worked on only after the requester's identity is verified.
  - A request must be answered within 30 days, and that deadline can be extended once by up to 60 days.
  - A breach must be reported to the regulator within 72 hours of being detected. It closes only once the actions taken are recorded.
  - Reports show requests by type and processing by lawful basis.
- **Environmental monitoring & permits**:
  - A permit is active only with a unique permit number and an expiry date, and it expires by itself after that date.
  - A sample that has a result and a limit is marked compliant or non-compliant automatically.
  - Each permit tracks its samples, exceedances and compliance rate.
  - Home shows the latest exceedances.
  - Reports show permits by type, samples by parameter and exceedances by month.
- **Parliament / council business**:
  - A bill moves through its readings and committee one stage at a time, and is voted on only at its second reading.
  - Motions, questions, petitions and reports can be voted on as soon as they are tabled.
  - A vote passes on a simple majority.
  - A sitting is held only once its day has come. When a sitting is adjourned, its undecided business carries forward.
  - Home shows the order paper and bills in progress.
  - Reports show votes, business by type and business by sponsor.
- **Prison & correctional records**:
  - Every inmate has one inmate number. A sentenced inmate has a sentence and a release date, and nobody is released before that date.
  - Visits are booked only for inmates in custody and need the visitor's ID.
  - An inmate can have one visit a day, and a visit is completed only on or after its day.
  - Each inmate tracks days in custody, days to release and visits.
  - Reports show population by cell, admissions and releases by month, and visits by month.
