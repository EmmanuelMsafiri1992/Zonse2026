title: Email, SMS, social media, websites, promo codes and SEO get real rules
---

- **Email marketing**:
  - List names are unique, and a list with scheduled campaigns can't be archived.
  - A campaign is scheduled or sent only to an active list with subscribers, and its email must carry an {unsubscribe} link. Sending counts the recipients, and scheduled campaigns go out on their send date each morning.
  - Opens can't exceed the recipients, and clicks can't exceed the opens. Automation steps read like "Day 0: Welcome", in day order.
  - Reports show results by campaign and by list.
- **SMS & WhatsApp campaigns**:
  - SMS templates are counted in billable parts: 160 characters for one part, or 153 per part for longer messages, and 70 or 67 once a message has emoji or other non-GSM characters.
  - WhatsApp variables are numbered {{1}}, {{2}} with no gaps, and editing an approved WhatsApp template sends it back for approval.
  - A campaign needs recipients and an approved template on its own channel. WhatsApp always needs one.
  - Sending counts the messages it takes. Recording delivery works out the delivery rate and the cost per delivered message, shown by channel in reports.
- **Social media scheduling & inbox**:
  - Networks are read however they are typed ("fb & insta"), and Instagram and TikTok posts need an image or video.
  - A scheduled post goes out on its date. Published posts record reach and engagement.
  - Replying to an inbox message times the response. Reports show the engagement rate per post and the average response time by network.
- **Website & landing pages**:
  - Slugs are written the web way and are unique. Landing pages and blog posts need an SEO description of at most 160 characters before they go live.
  - Form submissions need an email or phone and must come from a published page. A second open submission from the same person is refused, and messages stuffed with links are marked spam.
  - Converting a submission finds or creates the customer contact. Reports show submission and conversion rates by page.
- **Event marketing & promo codes**:
  - Promotions go live and end on their dates each morning, expiring their codes.
  - Codes are unique however they are typed, can't outlive their promotion, and take 1–100% or a fixed amount off.
  - Redeeming a code works out the discount, adds the sale to the promotion's revenue and uses the code up at its limit. Reports show the return on each promotion's budget.
- **SEO & analytics dashboard**:
  - Keywords are tracked once each.
  - Recording a new position keeps the previous one and a history of the last twelve readings, and the biggest movers are shown on the home page. Estimated visits come from the monthly searches and the usual click share for the position.
  - Short codes are unique, and click counts only go up.
