title: Water tanker and borehole, pay TV and airtime reseller apps get real rules
---

- **Borehole & water tanker delivery**: an order is for up to 40,000 litres and is dispatched with a tanker and driver. A tanker can't be out on two deliveries at once. Delivery is recorded on the day, and payment follows delivery. Borehole jobs move forward through their stages and can't go back: casing needs the depth drilled, the pump needs the yield and pump type, and completion needs the water quality result. The reports show litres delivered by tanker and every completed borehole's depth and yield.
- **Cable / satellite TV subscriptions**: a decoder or smartcard number is 8 to 14 digits and belongs to one subscriber. Activating a subscriber sets the renewal date a month ahead, and renewing adds months at the monthly price. Subscribers past their renewal date are suspended each morning and disconnected after 60 days unpaid. An installation needs at least 60% signal, and installing it activates the subscriber.
- **Airtime & bundle reseller**: each provider's float is the top-ups received less the successful sales, and a sale can't be more than the float left. Airtime and data go to a valid phone number, and electricity token sales keep the token number. Commission is worked out at the product's usual rate unless you enter it, and failed or reversed sales earn none. A sale can be reversed on the day it was made, which puts the amount back on the float. The home page shows each provider's float and flags any under 500.
