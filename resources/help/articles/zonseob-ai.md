## What it is

Zonseob AI is Claude, the AI model from Anthropic, available from the **Zonseob AI** page in your account. Ask it to write or edit anything, explain something, plan, calculate, translate or answer everyday questions. It runs on Zonseob's own Anthropic account, so you do not need your own account with Anthropic or a card that works abroad.

It is different from the workspace **Assistant**, which looks at your own records and needs an AI account set up in assistant settings. Zonseob AI cannot see your workspace data.

## Tokens

Every answer is paid for with tokens from your balance. New accounts start with free tokens so you can try it. An answer costs the tokens Claude reads (your question and the chat so far) plus four tokens for every token it writes, so a short question and answer is usually a few hundred tokens and a long document a few thousand. Each answer shows what it cost.

Tokens belong to you, not to a workspace: they stay with you in every workspace you use, they never expire, and you keep them even if a workspace's plan lapses.

## Buying tokens

Open [Zonseob AI](route:ai.index) and choose a pack under **Buy tokens**. Prices are in US dollars and the page shows what each pack costs in your currency. Pay with Paynow (EcoCash, OneMoney, InnBucks, ZimSwitch or a local card), with an international card, or by bank transfer or mobile money where account details are shown. Card and Paynow purchases are added to your balance the moment the payment is confirmed; transfers are confirmed by our team within a working day.

## Good to know

- Chats are private to you. Delete one from its page whenever you like.
- Nothing is charged when Claude cannot answer.
- Check facts and figures before relying on them, as with any AI.
