Most apps keep a list of records, such as patients, vehicles or orders.

## Add a record

Open the app from the menu and click **New**. Required fields are marked.

## Find a record

Use the search box and filters at the top of the list.

## Your own fields

Need to keep something extra? Add [custom fields](route:settings.custom-fields.index).

## Lots of records at once

You can [import a spreadsheet](help:importing-data) into most apps.
