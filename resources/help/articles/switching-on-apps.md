## Switch an app on

Open [Apps & modules](route:settings.modules.index), find the app and click **Switch on**. It appears in the menu straight away.

## Switch an app off

Click **Switch off** on the same page. The app disappears from the menu but its records are kept, so switching it back on later brings everything back.

## Which apps can I use?

Your plan decides how many apps you can switch on. See [Plans and billing](help:plans-and-billing).
