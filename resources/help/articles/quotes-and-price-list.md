## Your price list

Add the things you sell under [Items](route:items.index). Each item has a price and an optional tax rate, so invoice lines fill themselves in.

## Quotes

Create a quote from [Quotes](route:quotes.index). When the customer accepts, convert it into an invoice in one click. Nothing has to be typed twice.
