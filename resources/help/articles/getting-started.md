Welcome! Your workspace holds everything for one business: your customers, sales, team and apps.

## 1. Check your business details

Open [Workspace settings](route:settings.workspace.edit) and confirm your business name, currency and time zone. These appear on every invoice and receipt.

## 2. Switch on the apps you need

Go to [Apps & modules](route:settings.modules.index) and switch on what your business uses. Each app adds its own menu item. You can switch an app off again at any time, and its data is kept.

## 3. Bring in your customers

Add contacts one at a time, or [import a spreadsheet](help:importing-data) to move your whole list across.

## 4. Invite your team

See [Inviting your team](help:inviting-your-team).
