## The menu

The menu on the left lists every app you have switched on. Click the menu button at the top to collapse it.

## Search

The search box at the top finds customers, invoices, records and more. Press **/** to jump to it.

## Notifications

The bell shows what needs your attention, such as an approval waiting for you or an invoice that was paid. Open [all notifications](route:notifications.index) to see older ones.

## More than one business

Click the workspace name at the top to switch between businesses or start a new one. Each workspace has its own data, team and plan.

## Guided tours

Some pages have a short tour. Open the help menu (the life-buoy icon) and choose **Take the tour of this page**.
