## Change plan

Open [Plan & billing](route:settings.billing.index) and choose a plan. Upgrades apply straight away; downgrades apply at the end of the period.

## Receipts

Every payment has a receipt you can download from the same page.

## Only owners

Only the workspace owner and admins can see billing.
