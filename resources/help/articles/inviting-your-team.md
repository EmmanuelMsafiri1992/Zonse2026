## Invite someone

1. Open [Team members](route:settings.members.index).
2. Click **Invite**, enter their email address and choose a role.
3. They get an email with a link to join.

## Roles

- **Owner**: everything, including billing and closing the workspace.
- **Admin**: everything except ownership.
- **Member**: day-to-day work in the apps.
- **Viewer**: can look but not change.

## Remove someone

Remove a person from the same page. Their past work stays in the records and the [audit log](route:settings.audit.index).
