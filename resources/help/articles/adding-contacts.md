Contacts are shared by every app, so a customer you add once can be invoiced, booked and messaged.

## Add a contact

1. Open [Contacts](route:contacts.index).
2. Click **New contact**.
3. Fill in a name or a company name. Everything else is optional.

## Types

Each contact is a **customer**, **supplier** or **lead**. Use the filters at the top of the list to see one type at a time.

## Bring your list across

Moving from a spreadsheet or another system? See [Importing data](help:importing-data).

## Export

Use **Export** on the contacts list to download everyone as a spreadsheet.
