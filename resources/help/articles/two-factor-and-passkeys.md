## Two-step sign-in

Open [My profile](route:profile.edit) and turn on two-step sign-in. After your password you will also enter a code from an authenticator app. Keep the recovery codes somewhere safe.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of a password. Add one from [My profile](route:profile.edit).

## Activity

Admins can see sign-ins and changes in the [audit log](route:settings.audit.index).
