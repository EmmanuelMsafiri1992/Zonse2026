## Create an invoice

1. Open [Invoices](route:invoices.index) and click **New invoice**.
2. Pick the customer. New customers can be added from the same screen.
3. Add lines from your price list or type them in.
4. Save as a draft, or save and send.

## Send it

Send the invoice by email from the invoice page. Your customer gets a link to view and download it as a PDF.

## Record a payment

When money arrives, open the invoice and click **Record payment**. Part payments are fine; the balance updates automatically.

## Overdue invoices

The numbers at the top of the invoices list show what is outstanding and overdue.
