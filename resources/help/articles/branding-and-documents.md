## Business details

Your name, address and tax number come from [Workspace settings](route:settings.workspace.edit).

## Logo and colours

Upload your logo and choose your colours under [Branding](route:settings.branding.edit). They are used on documents, emails and the client portal.

## Invoice layout

Change numbering, terms and the invoice design under [Invoice settings](route:settings.invoicing.edit).
