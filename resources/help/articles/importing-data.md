## Start an import

1. Open [Import data](route:settings.imports.index).
2. Choose what you are importing and upload a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file. The first row must hold the column headings.
3. Match each column to a field. Exports from QuickBooks, Sage and Xero are matched for you.
4. Check the preview, then click **Import**.

## Duplicates

Choose whether rows that match an existing record are skipped or update it.

## Made a mistake?

Click **Undo** on the finished import to remove everything it added.

## Need a starting point?

Download the Excel or CSV template from the import page.
